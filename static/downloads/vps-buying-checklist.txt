AIProFreelancer — VPS buying checklist (2026-10-02)

[ ] Define the application and supported operating system.
[ ] Record the provider and exact plan.
[ ] Record checkout term, renewal cost and add-on charges.
[ ] Verify IP/network configuration and permitted workload.
[ ] Confirm management and support scope in writing.
[ ] Read payment, cancellation and refund terms before paying.
[ ] Check account information and verification requirements.
[ ] Do not infer anonymity from cryptocurrency billing.
[ ] Check content permissions and the actual abuse policy.
[ ] Plan backups and test restoring a safe copy.
[ ] Keep credentials out of public files and logs.
[ ] Test a real application page, domain and HTTPS after deployment.
[ ] Record application measurements before making performance claims.

No provider rating, benchmark, discount or profit guarantee is implied.
