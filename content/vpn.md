Choose a VPN around the connection you need for your work. Start with your devices, the networks you use and the access rules for client systems. This directory brings together our NordVPN review, provider comparisons and practical connection guides. Read the review when you are considering a subscription; use a comparison when you already have two providers on your shortlist.

For a freelancer, the useful question is whether the service fits an ordinary working session. List your laptop, phone and other devices, then identify any client application that needs a fixed or approved connection. Check whether the plan covers your setup and whether switching networks interrupts the work you need to finish. Our guides provide a way to record those requirements before committing to a longer subscription.
