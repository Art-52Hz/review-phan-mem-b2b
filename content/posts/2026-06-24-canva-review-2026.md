## Quick Verdict

Choose by the deliverable: required dimensions, export format, client editing access and the assets included in the selected plan. We have no reproducible scored test supporting a numeric rating.

---

## What to test in the editor

Use the chosen plan to test one representative brief. For generated copy, verify facts; for layouts, inspect spacing and branding; for image edits, inspect edges and repaired areas at full resolution. Record manual corrections. We have no controlled evidence that these outputs outperform competing tools.

## Canva Pricing 2026

The [official pricing page](https://www.canva.com/pricing/), checked October 8, 2026, lists Free, Pro, Business and Enterprise. It separates individual and team requirements, shared AI allowances and optional paid AI add-ons. A paid plan does not mean unlimited use of every AI tool.

Check the current regional price, taxes, billing term, required seats and trial conditions before committing. The previous Teams table and claim that Pro included the full AI suite were not a reliable description of the current offer.

| Required work | Verify before purchase |
|---|---|
| Static social graphics | Dimensions, usable assets and final export format |
| Recurring branded work | Brand settings and who can edit shared designs |
| Several reviewers | Seats, permissions and approval needs |
| AI-heavy production | Allowance consumption, fair-use rules and extra charges |
| Client handoff | Client access and the ability to edit included elements |

Use the [project-cost worksheet](/posts/freelance-tool-project-cost/) to include revisions, export checks and handoff time alongside the subscription.

## Compare the same deliverable

Repeat the same brief with the same source assets and acceptance criteria in each candidate tool. Compare active work time, revisions, export fidelity and client editing access. Do not choose a winner from subscription price or a template preview alone.

## 3 Proposed Workflows to Test

### 1. The freelancer building a client's social presence
Create one website cover and one social graphic from a fictional brief. Inspect mobile readability, cropping and export dimensions. Measure active work and revisions before estimating a full batch; no time saving is established here.

### 2. The small e-commerce store
Use an authorised product photo and check whether editing preserves the real product's appearance. Record manual corrections and seek approval before publication. This is a proposed exercise, not evidence that professional photography is unnecessary.

### 3. The consultant making a pitch deck
Create a short sample deck and test its export using the client's intended software. Check text, objects, fonts and notes. Record what the client can edit; no measured completion time is claimed.

---

## Client handoff

Agree who owns the source design and account, which exports are required, and how later revisions will be handled. Keep asset sources and the approved version together. A final image and an editable design are different deliverables.

## Final Verdict

Test one representative design before upgrading: inspect mobile readability, cropping, dimensions, watermarks and the client's editing access. Keep the source design, exports and approval record together. This is a proposed acceptance check, not a completed benchmark or a promise of time savings.

Read [Canva's licensing explanation](https://www.canva.com/licensing-explained/) for the intended content use. It distinguishes Free and Pro content and includes restrictions for redistribution, standalone content and trademarks. A subscription does not remove those distinctions.
