## Key Features

### Free Plan — The Best in the Category
Copy.ai's free plan is genuinely usable. You get:
- 2,000 words per month
- Access to 90+ templates
- 1 user seat
- No credit card required

For occasional copywriting tasks — an ad, an email, a few social posts — the free tier handles it without requiring a subscription commitment. This is the main reason Copy.ai still wins against tools like Writesonic (which only offers a 7-day trial).

### 90+ Marketing Templates
Copy.ai has one of the largest template libraries in the space:
- Facebook Ads (multiple formats)
- Google Ads headlines and descriptions
- Email subject lines and body copy
- Blog intros, outlines, bullets-to-blog
- LinkedIn posts and Twitter threads
- Product descriptions
- Sales page copy
- Video scripts

### Workflows — Multi-Step Content Automation
This is Copy.ai's most underrated feature. Workflows let you chain AI prompts together — input a product name, and the workflow can automatically generate a blog outline, then expand it into sections, then add SEO meta description, all in one run. For agencies producing content at scale, this is a meaningful workflow unlock.

### Chat Interface (Powered by Claude and GPT-4)
Copy.ai's chat uses multiple underlying models (Claude, GPT-4) depending on your task. The output diversity is useful — different tasks genuinely benefit from different models.

### Brand Voice (Pro+)
On paid plans, you can save brand voice settings and style guides. It's functional but less sophisticated than Jasper's brand voice training system.

## Pros and Cons

### What Copy.ai Does Well

- **Best free tier in AI writing** — 2,000 words/month, real templates, no card required
- **90+ templates** — more template variety than Jasper or Writesonic
- **Workflows are genuinely powerful** — multi-step automation that competitors don't match at this price

### Where It Falls Short

- **Long-form article quality** — for 2,000+ word SEO articles, Writesonic produces better-structured drafts
- **No real-time web search** — Copy.ai can't pull live data the way Chatsonic (Writesonic) can

- **Free plan limits** — 2,000 words/month sounds generous but runs out quickly for regular users
- **UI changes frequently** — the interface has been redesigned multiple times, which frustrates returning users

## FAQ

**Is Copy.ai really free?**
Yes. The free plan gives you 2,000 words/month with access to 90+ templates and no credit card required. It's not a trial — it's a permanent free tier. For occasional use, it's fully functional.

**How does Copy.ai compare to Jasper AI?**
Copy.ai is better for teams on a budget, short-form copy, and workflow automation. Jasper is better for brand voice consistency, enterprise teams, and structured long-form content. Jasper costs $20 more per month at the base tier.

**Can Copy.ai write full blog posts?**
Yes, but it's not its strongest use case. The Blog Wizard and bullets-to-blog templates produce a usable first draft, but for SEO-optimized long-form articles, Writesonic or Jasper produce better results.

**Does Copy.ai have a plagiarism checker?**
No built-in checker. Use Grammarly or Copyscape separately.
