Before choosing Copy.ai, decide whether you need a writing conversation or a repeatable process that combines inputs, generation and delivery. A chat allowance does not establish the cost or availability of a workflow.

**Review scope, October 8, 2026:** This is a documentation-based evaluation guide. Earlier numerical ratings, competitor rankings and permanent-free-plan promises have been removed because we do not have sufficient retained evidence for them. We have not completed a controlled product benchmark.

## Check chat and workflow allowances separately

The [official pricing page](https://www.copy.ai/prices) separates chat access from workflow credit allowances. It explains that workflow consumption varies with the work performed and that a run's side panel shows credits used. Check the current plan and your account rather than assuming unlimited chat means unlimited workflow runs.

| Question | Evidence to collect before paying |
|---|---|
| What task are you buying for? | A specific client brief and required deliverable |
| Is chat sufficient? | An acceptable sample produced in the intended editor |
| Is a workflow required? | Available actions, inputs, outputs and plan access |
| What does a run consume? | Credits recorded for a representative run |
| What happens after revision? | Consumption for reruns and manual checking time |
| Who will use it? | Required users, permissions and total checkout cost |

Do not assume an older free tier, model list or fixed monthly price still applies. Confirm current access and billing before starting a client project.

## Test one brief before building a pipeline

Use a non-confidential brief you own. Define the audience, approved facts, style, format and prohibited claims. Keep that brief fixed while you test so a changed instruction does not masquerade as improved output.

1. Produce one draft and save the original output.
2. Check every factual statement against the approved source material.
3. Record missing requirements, unsupported additions and voice changes.
4. Revise the result and measure the human review time.
5. If automation is required, verify how the approved result reaches the delivery system and who checks it before release.

A plausible draft is not publication approval. For repeated work, keep a named reviewer and a recoverable record of inputs, versions and final acceptance.

## Budget for accepted work

Record the actual subscription commitment, required users, measured run consumption, extra usage if applicable and manual revisions. Use several representative tasks before extrapolating to a monthly workload.

| Task | Runs and reruns | Credits observed | Review minutes | Accepted deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Client email | Record | Record | Record | Yes / no |
| Product description | Record | Record | Record | Yes / no |
| Article outline | Record | Record | Record | Yes / no |

This is a worksheet, not measured Copy.ai performance. A low cost per generated draft may still produce a high cost per accepted deliverable if revisions dominate.
