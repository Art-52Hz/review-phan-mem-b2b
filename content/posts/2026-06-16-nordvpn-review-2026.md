NordVPN is a candidate to evaluate when you need a VPN for your own device and an approved work setup. Decide from the connection requirements and the full subscription commitment, rather than a discount headline.

**Review scope, October 9, 2026:** This guide uses the vendor's current pricing page and an original acceptance checklist. We do not have retained evidence for the previous six-month testing claim, speed table, streaming results or comparative winner. Those claims have been removed. Links to NordVPN below are ordinary official links; no verified affiliate referral is included.

## Compare the complete invoice

The [official pricing page](https://nordvpn.com/pricing/), checked October 9, displayed Basic, Complete and Ultimate Max in the offer returned to us. It separated the first subscription term from annual renewal and said VAT may apply. The displayed currency was euros; this is not a universal US-dollar quotation.

Do not copy an old monthly-equivalent price into a client budget. Record the actual offer available to your account and country:

| Cost or condition | Record before deciding |
| --- | --- |
| Subscription term | Monthly, annual or longer commitment |
| First payment | Full amount charged, currency and tax |
| Renewal | Amount, renewal interval and date |
| Included features | What the selected package actually covers |
| Optional services | Whether dedicated IP or other extras add cost |
| Cancellation and refund | Applicable conditions and steps |

The page advertises a money-back guarantee, but a paid subscription with refund conditions is not the same thing as a free trial. Read the linked offer terms before relying on reimbursement. This review does not buy or enable a subscription.

## Check the work requirement first

Ask the client or employer whether a consumer VPN is permitted for the intended connection. A consumer subscription does not automatically provide access to company resources or replace the organization's approved access system.

List the devices and applications you actually use. Check support for each operating system, the selected plan's connection allowance and any device-specific limitations in current vendor documentation. Do not assume a feature on one app works identically on another.

For shared client systems, agree who owns the account, handles renewal and assists if access stops. Do not share credentials simply to avoid a separate account.

## A connection acceptance record

Use a non-sensitive website and your own approved network for an initial check. Avoid testing with client secrets, a live financial transaction or an important meeting.

1. Record the device, operating system, app version, date and chosen connection settings.
2. Check that ordinary pages load and the applications you need remain usable.
3. Read the app's documented disconnect and kill-switch behavior.
4. Check reconnection using the provider's instructions and a harmless test page.
5. Record any application exceptions, local-network restrictions or unexpected behavior.
6. Keep a recovery route that follows the client or platform's approved process.

This is a proposed evaluation process, not a test we have completed. A connection icon alone does not establish that every application is protected.

For the difference between HTTPS, VPN tunnels and account safeguards, see our [VPN connection checklist](/posts/best-vpn-for-crypto-trading-2026/). Its platform-specific Proton example should not be treated as NordVPN app documentation.

## How to compare performance honestly

If responsiveness matters, compare the same ordinary workload on the same device and network, at recorded times, with and without the VPN. Keep the selected server and protocol in the record. Repeat observations before inferring a pattern.

Do not treat one download result as a universal speed ranking. This page contains no measured latency, throughput, leak-test or streaming-access results. Server counts and marketing claims do not establish performance on your connection.

## When to defer the purchase

Keep the decision pending if the client's rules prohibit the setup, the application you need cannot work with it, renewal cost is unclear or recovery depends on unsupported assumptions.

Use the [whole-project cost checklist](/posts/freelance-tool-project-cost/) to include your setup and support time alongside the subscription. A low introductory payment can still create a larger commitment than the project needs.
