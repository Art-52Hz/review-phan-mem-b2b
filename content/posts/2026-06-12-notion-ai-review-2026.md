## Key Features in 2026

### Ask Notion — Most Useful Feature
Ask Notion lets you query your entire workspace in plain language. Instead of hunting through pages, you ask "What did we decide about the Q3 launch?" and get an answer with links back to the source pages. It searches across pages, wikis, databases, and integrations like Google Drive and Slack.

For users with large Notion workspaces, this is the single best reason to pay for AI. Finding information across hundreds of pages used to take minutes; Ask Notion does it in seconds.

### AI Writing Assistance
Standard AI writing features built into every page: generate text, summarize, rewrite, fix grammar, translate, make shorter/longer, change tone. These work well for quick edits and summaries. They're not a replacement for dedicated AI writing tools for longer content, but for in-context edits while working in Notion they're genuinely convenient.

### Meeting Notes (AI Meeting Notes)
Notion AI can connect to your calendar and join meetings automatically to generate transcripts and summaries. For people who take a lot of client calls or team meetings, this saves significant time — one feature that pays for the $10/month add-on on its own if you use it consistently.

### Custom Agents (New in 2026)
Notion's biggest 2026 launch: autonomous agents that run multi-step workflows on a schedule or trigger. Examples: a morning digest agent that pulls your tasks and meetings each day, a pipeline agent that monitors a database and sends Slack alerts, a weekly review agent that summarizes activity. 

**The catch:** Custom Agents use credits. Starting May 2026, credits cost $10 per 1,000. A simple daily morning summary agent can consume 3,000 credits/month — that's $30 on top of your existing plan. This is a significant pricing change that has frustrated users.

### Extract Action Items from Documents
Paste a meeting transcript, a long email, or a document, and Notion AI extracts action items, key decisions, and summaries. Useful but not unique — ChatGPT does this too.

## FAQ

**Is Notion AI free?**
Notion AI is not free on Free or Plus plans — it's a $10/month add-on. Business plan users ($20/user/month) get AI included at no extra cost.

**Can Notion AI write blog posts?**
It can generate blog drafts, but the output quality is below specialized tools like Writesonic or Jasper AI for SEO-optimized long-form content. Best used for internal documents, summaries, and quick rewrites rather than publish-ready content.

**Is Ask Notion better than ChatGPT for finding information?**
For your own workspace, yes — Ask Notion understands your specific context and cites sources. For general questions or research, ChatGPT is more capable.

**Should I pay for Notion AI if I'm on the Free plan?**
Only if you actively use Ask Notion or meeting notes features regularly. For occasional use, ChatGPT free tier is more versatile at no cost.
