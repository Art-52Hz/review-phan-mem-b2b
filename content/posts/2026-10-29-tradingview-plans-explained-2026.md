## Which one should you pick?

- **You are learning charts, check markets occasionally, and have fewer than three active price alerts:** stay on Basic. Pay only after you can name a restriction that interrupts your regular routine.
- **You follow a short list and need two charts side by side or around 20 price and technical alerts:** choose Essential. It is the lowest-cost paid fit for that workload at today's annual special price.
- **Your alert condition needs multiple conditions, or you routinely need four charts and close to 100 alerts:** choose Plus. TradingView's [multi-condition alert guide](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000761492-multi-condition-alerts/?aff_id=1173587), checked November 23, 2026, says the builder starts at Plus; verify that your script is supported before subscribing.
- **You monitor many setups at once and actually use 8 charts, 25 indicators per chart or hundreds of alerts:** choose Premium. If the extra capacity would sit idle, Plus costs $839.40 less over 12 months at the current annual special prices.
- **You are a professional user:** check eligibility before choosing; TradingView says only Ultimate is available to professional users.

## Separate data, webhook and execution costs

A paid chart plan does not automatically include real-time quotes from every exchange. Check the market-data status for the exact symbols you use and add an exchange subscription only if required. TradingView's official [additional market data guide](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000471705-how-to-purchase-additional-market-data/?aff_id=1173587), checked November 23, 2026, explains how to review and purchase those feeds.

A webhook alert sends a message to an external application; it does not provide the receiver or guarantee that an order executes. TradingView's [webhook configuration guide](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000529348-how-to-configure-webhook-alerts/?aff_id=1173587), checked November 23, 2026, says two-factor authentication is required and documents the outgoing request. ## FAQ

### Is TradingView Basic enough for one crypto price alert?

Usually, yes: Basic includes 3 active price alerts. A Reddit user with one coin and continuous price-change alert needs should first define the trigger; TradingView supports price alerts, while notifications do not turn every tiny movement into a sensible trade signal. See the [one-coin alert discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/TradingView/comments/1e2sk82/please_help_me_choose_the_right_trading_view/).

### Which plan allows multi-condition alerts?

Plus and higher. Essential has price and technical alerts, but the official multi-condition alert builder starts at Plus. Check compatibility if the alert depends on a community indicator or a script using alert().

### Is Premium worth it if I mostly want more charts?

Only if you routinely use the additional chart slots or alert capacity. Premium's current annual special price is $719.40 for the first year, versus $359.40 for Plus; try arranging your real workspace on Plus before paying the extra $360 in year one.

### Does TradingView annual billing renew at the same discounted price?

Do not assume it does. The pricing page labels the current annual prices special offers and displays savings against regular annual prices. Confirm the renewal amount shown to your account before checkout; the 24 and 36 month table above uses the implied regular rate after year one.

### Do I have to buy exchange data with a paid plan?

No single data add-on is mandatory for every customer. Some exchange quotes are separate subscriptions. Check the feed and exchange for the symbols you trade; Basic or a paid chart plan does not determine every exchange's data charge.

### Can I cancel an annual subscription and get unused months refunded?

Cancellation prevents the next renewal but normally leaves access through the paid billing period. TradingView's policy lists a limited refund window and conditions; read the current [official payment and cancellation terms](https://www.tradingview.com/policies/?aff_id=1173587) before paying.
