## Key Features Deep Dive

### Funnel Builder

Drag-and-drop editor, pre-built templates for lead capture pages, sales pages, order pages, upsell pages. Not as polished as ClickFunnels aesthetically, but functionally complete. You can build a full product funnel (optin → sales page → order form → upsell → thank you) in about 2 hours.

**What works:** One-click upsells, order bumps, A/B testing on paid plans, custom domains on all plans.

**What doesn't:** The template library is smaller than competitors. If you need pixel-perfect design, you'll spend time customizing.

### Email Marketing

Unlimited emails on all plans — including free. You can build campaigns, automations, and tag-based segmentation. The interface is clean but less powerful than ActiveCampaign or Klaviyo for complex sequences.

**Good for:** Nurture sequences, broadcast emails, simple automations triggered by funnel actions.

**Not ideal for:** Advanced behavioral segmentation, e-commerce-specific flows.

### Course Platform

Host video courses with lesson drip, member area, certificates, and payment integration. Students get a clean portal. Comparable to Teachable's basic tier.

### Affiliate Program

This is where Systeme.io genuinely stands out — you can run your **own** affiliate program for your own products, built into the platform. Set commission rates, create affiliate links for each product, see who referred who. No third-party affiliate software needed.
