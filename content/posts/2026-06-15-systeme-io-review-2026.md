## Free plan limits: what can stop you first?

The easy mistake is to watch only contact growth. Free also limits you to one automation rule, one email campaign and one custom domain, according to the [official Free plan FAQ](https://systeme.io/pricing?sa=sa0240953427819ac8307d665a325f6afb57632d70), checked November 4, 2026.

For a simple resource funnel, that can be enough: collect permission, deliver the download and follow up with a relevant offer. It becomes restrictive when you want separate onboarding journeys for different products. Sketch those journeys before importing an audience. A small list with several offers may outgrow the automation allowance before it outgrows the contact allowance.

Download the [blank lead-funnel checklist](/downloads/freelancer-lead-funnel-checklist.txt) and adapt it to your audience and product. Write down the entry page, promised resource, follow-up and purchase destination. Filling out this worksheet does not establish that the funnel works.

There is an inconsistency on the pricing page: its benefits section says unlimited students, while its Free FAQ mentions 500 students. If learner capacity determines your purchase, obtain confirmation from support rather than planning around the more generous statement. Source: [pricing benefits and Free FAQ](https://systeme.io/pricing?sa=sa0240953427819ac8307d665a325f6afb57632d70), checked November 4, 2026.

## When is a paid plan worth it?

### Startup: expand a working offer

Startup is my first paid recommendation when Free cannot accommodate your real product lineup or audience. Keep a short upgrade note: what is blocked, which allowance fixes it, and when you will use that allowance. If you cannot name the blocked task, continue with Free.

A creator preparing another course should count every course that must remain available, including older purchases. Do not remove customer access just to squeeze a launch into a cheaper plan. Similarly, list cleanup should remove unwanted records, not consenting readers who still want your emails.

### Webinar: add an evergreen presentation

Choose Webinar when an evergreen presentation is central to your offer or Startup capacity no longer fits. Before buying, outline the presentation, registration message, follow-up and product. A webinar feature cannot rescue an unclear offer or generate an audience by itself.

If your launch uses a live meeting hosted elsewhere, do not assume the plan name makes this upgrade necessary. Identify what the built-in workflow would replace and whether you will use it regularly.

### Unlimited: remove an operational bottleneck

Shortlist Unlimited when smaller tiers would force you to split a working business across accounts or remove useful assets. It should simplify an operation you already have. Do not buy it merely because a growth forecast looks attractive.

## Who should choose Systeme.io, and who should not?

**Choose it** if you are a solo creator or affiliate publisher who wants a connected landing page, permission-based follow-up and digital offer. The strongest reason to try it is to reduce the number of tools involved in a simple customer journey.

**Stay with Free** while learning which resource attracts the right readers and which product they want. Spending on capacity before you have a usable offer adds cost without solving that uncertainty.

**Investigate alternatives first** if your purchase depends on a specialist integration, complex access controls or a precise learner allowance. Make that requirement your first acceptance check. A feature list does not establish compatibility with existing systems.

The most concrete downside is that exceeding allowances can interrupt operations. Systeme.io documents warnings and restrictions when contact or feature limits are exceeded, including after a downgrade. Review the plan summary before reducing your subscription. Source: [official account restriction process](https://help.systeme.io/article/1086?sa=sa0240953427819ac8307d665a325f6afb57632d70), checked November 4, 2026.

Unlimited sending does not establish inbox placement. Authenticate the sending domain and use a permission-based list before evaluating performance. The official email-volume guide cited above recommends domain authentication and list hygiene. Its published account results are vendor evidence, not a forecast for your list.

## How to test it yourself in 15 minutes

This is a suggested initial check, not a promise that a production funnel takes 15 minutes to build.

1. **Define the journey.** Choose a resource you own and a relevant product. Write the promise and next action in plain English.
2. **Build a draft page.** Add a signup form to a template and inspect the mobile preview. Request only necessary personal data.
3. **Connect delivery.** Configure the follow-up to deliver what the page promises. Use your own test address rather than a purchased or scraped list.
4. **Walk through as a subscriber.** Check confirmation, resource delivery and unsubscribe. Record missing messages or duplicate triggers.
5. **Check the purchase path.** Preview your own product journey without a live charge. For affiliate offers, verify destination and traffic permissions without self-clicking a referral link.
6. **Write the decision.** Record what worked, what failed and the allowance needed to resolve a blocker. Keep Free unless upgrading solves that blocker.

## FAQ

These questions follow the public pricing FAQs and restriction documentation cited above; they are not claimed as Search Console queries.

### Is Systeme.io really free, or is it a trial?

It is a permanent free tier with capacity limits. Use it to validate the signup-to-delivery journey before subscribing. Free is the recommended starting point for an unproven offer.

### Which free-plan limit should I check first?

Check your workflow, then its allowances. Contact count is only part of the decision: multiple products may need separate follow-up journeys even when your list is small.

### When should I upgrade to Startup?

Upgrade when a needed task exceeds Free and Startup covers it. Keep a written reason for the purchase so you can review whether the added capacity is being used.

### Do I need Webinar to sell a course?

No. The comparison above includes course capacity on lower tiers. Choose Webinar for evergreen webinar functionality or higher allowances rather than assuming every course needs a webinar funnel.

### Can I cancel or downgrade later?

The pricing FAQ says cancellation keeps the current plan until the next billing date, then returns the account to Free. Review restrictions before downgrading an account with assets above the lower allowance. Source: [official cancellation and downgrade FAQ](https://systeme.io/pricing?sa=sa0240953427819ac8307d665a325f6afb57632d70), checked November 4, 2026.
