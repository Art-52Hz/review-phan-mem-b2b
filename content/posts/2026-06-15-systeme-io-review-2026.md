## What Is Systeme.io?

Systeme.io is an all-in-one online business platform built by Aurélien Amacker (launched 2018). It combines:

- **Sales funnel builder** — landing pages, upsells, order bumps
- **Email marketing** — campaigns, automations, list management
- **Online courses** — host and sell video courses
- **Affiliate program management** — run your own affiliate program
- **Blog** — built-in blogging (no Hugo required)
- **Automation workflows** — trigger-based sequences

The buying question is whether one account can support your actual workflow. Compare total cost, maintenance and data export rather than adding historical competitor prices.

## Key Features Deep Dive

### Funnel Builder

Define one resource and one next action. For a presentation freelancer, that might be a slide-handoff checklist followed by an invitation to enquire about a redesign. Check mobile layout, resource delivery and the client's ownership of the page. No setup-time benchmark has been measured for this guide.

### Email Marketing

The vendor lists email marketing and automation. Before using it, document necessary fields, consent wording, confirmation, unsubscribe and suppression behaviour. Verify the intended sequence without duplicate automation in a workflow you own or have permission to test. We have not measured inbox placement or compared automation performance against competitors.

### Course Platform

If you sell a course, evaluate learner access, delivery, support and export separately from a service-enquiry funnel. A listed course feature is not proof that it meets a particular client's needs.

### Affiliate Program

The pricing page lists affiliate management for your own products. This is separate from earning a commission by promoting Systeme.io itself. Review payout, attribution and account responsibilities for the workflow you actually operate.

## Systeme.io Affiliate Program (For Promoting Systeme.io Itself)

The vendor describes the following affiliate terms; a high rate does not establish suitability for the reader:

- **60% lifetime commissions** — subject to eligible transactions and the attribution rules below
- **No approval needed** — sign up and get your link instantly
- **Commission basis** — applicable VAT is deducted before the 60% calculation; no second-tier earnings are assumed here
- **Cookie duration** — 365 days

Affiliate income depends on eligible purchases and attribution. The official help page specifies a payout threshold above $30 and at least a 30-day hold, with payments on the 10th. Referral association after signup has an exception when another affiliate's payment-page link is used. These terms do not establish our account's eligibility or forecast income. See the [official affiliate help](https://help.systeme.io/article/162-how-the-systeme-io-affiliate-program-works).

## Systeme.io vs Alternatives

## FAQ

**Is Systeme.io really free?**
The current pricing FAQ advertises a free plan without expiry or credit card requirement. Verify current limits in the plan comparison and account before relying on them.

**How does Systeme.io compare to ClickFunnels?**
We have not completed a controlled comparison. Compare the current plans against your entry page, follow-up, client permissions and export requirements; historical prices alone cannot determine the better choice.

**Can I use Systeme.io to sell affiliate products?**
Check both the platform's content policies and each offer's permitted traffic rules before operating a campaign. Do not assume an offer allows every channel or treat resource consent as permission for unrelated email promotions.

**What's the affiliate commission for Systeme.io?**
The help page lists 60% lifetime commissions with applicable VAT deducted first. Eligibility, attribution, refunds and payout timing still determine actual cash; this is not a forecast for this website.

Evaluate the free plan if its current limits cover a small, defined workflow. Upgrade only when you can name the limit blocking an actual task and justify its full cost.
