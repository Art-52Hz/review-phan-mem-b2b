## The limits that can change your decision

Free includes 2,000 contacts, 3 funnels, 1 course, and 1 automation rule. The rule limit matters if buyers of different products need separate follow-up logic, even when your list is small. The main current pricing page lists Free course capacity as 500 students in its comparison; an all-plans summary separately says unlimited students. Since those statements conflict, confirm learner capacity with support before choosing based on it.

The paid tiers increase the available workflows, courses, and contacts; Webinar is the first tier whose current comparison includes evergreen webinars. Systeme.io also lists 0% platform transaction fees, but that is not the same as free card processing. Check the processor's own rate for your country and payment method before pricing a product.

### Is Systeme.io Free enough to sell a first offer?

It can be, if one course, three funnels, one automation rule, and 2,000 contacts cover the path from signup to delivery. Run a test purchase and course-access flow before inviting customers.

### Can I take payment on WordPress and host the course in Systeme.io?

That split setup is possible only if the payment event can trigger reliable account access. Verify the integration and test enrollment, confirmation email, and access removal before relying on it for customers.

### How much does Startup cost over two years?

At today's list price, two years cost $408 with monthly billing or $340 with two annual renewals. The annual amount is paid upfront each year.

### Does Systeme.io charge transaction fees?

The pricing page lists 0% Systeme.io transaction fees. Stripe, PayPal, or another payment provider can still charge its own processing fee.

### Should I choose annual billing to get free migration?

The pricing page advertises two months free with annual billing and free migration. If moving existing assets is the reason to pay upfront, ask support to confirm which assets qualify and the scope before subscribing.
