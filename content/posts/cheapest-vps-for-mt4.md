## Minimum specs: size the whole machine

OANDA's [MT4 system requirements](https://www.oanda.com/sg-en/skills-and-insights/education/platforms-and-tools/metatrader4/system-requirements-to-check-before-installing-mt4/), checked November 15, 2026, list a 1 GHz CPU and 512 MB RAM minimum for the terminal, and recommend 2 GHz or higher and at least 1 GB RAM. That is terminal guidance, not a Windows VPS recommendation. Windows, remote access, updates, security tools, charts and EAs also use resources.

For a small single-terminal setup, compare the actual EA workload on a demo install and watch whole-server memory and CPU. A terminal that opens does not prove it can run your charts, indicators and EA together after updates. For multiple terminals or memory-heavy EAs, shortlist more RAM only after checking the vendor's requirements and your observed load. ## Questions traders ask before buying

The questions below reflect recurring buyer questions in [a recent EA VPS discussion on Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/Forexstrategy/comments/1xp3saz/need_vps_for_ea/) and [a Windows VPS discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/VPS/comments/1r998z4/looking_for_a_cheap_vps_provider_for_a_windows/), viewed November 15, 2026. Forum replies are user reports, not provider specifications.

### Is 2 GB RAM enough for one MT4 EA?

It can be a starting candidate, not a guarantee. Check memory with your own charts, indicators and EA running, and leave room for Windows and updates. Move up if the actual workload approaches the limit.

### Can MT4 run on a Linux VPS?

MetaQuotes' [official MT4 Linux installation guide](https://www.metatrader4.com/en/trading-platform/help/userguide/install_linux), checked November 15, 2026, uses Wine. Broker builds, plug-ins and DLL-dependent EAs can differ, so verify your exact setup on a demo before paying for a long term.

### Does the cheapest Linux VPS include Windows?

No. Hostinger's KVM VPS templates are Linux. A Windows image or license is not included in that plan; do not compare its headline rate as a Windows VPS price.

### Should I choose a VPS near my broker?

Check your broker's trade-server location and available VPS regions before ordering. Provider marketing about low latency does not establish the route from your specific server to your broker.

### Can I get a free VPS for MT4?

Ask your broker whether your account balance or trading volume qualifies for its own VPS offer. Eligibility and minimum activity vary, so confirm the current terms with the broker before including it in your budget.

## Check before paying

Confirm the provider's exact OS image, billing term, renewal amount, included backup scope and any tax at checkout. Test the broker's MT4 build and EA on a demo, check the broker server region, and document how you will recover the terminal after reboot or failed updates.
