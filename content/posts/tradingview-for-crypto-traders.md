## A symbol mismatch can cost more than a plan limit

Before subscribing, write down the full symbol you use, including exchange, quote currency and whether it is spot or a perpetual/futures contract. For example, an exchange-specific BTC spot pair and a perpetual contract are different markets. Do not compare a TradingView chart with your order screen until both show the same instrument and timeframe.

If you already execute on an exchange, first see whether its chart covers your routine. Kraken's official chart documentation describes its candlestick and drawing tools; check the same symbol there before adding another subscription. Source: [Kraken chart and market view documentation](https://support.kraken.com/articles/201893698-trading-prices-and-charts-for-kraken), checked November 26, 2026. Choose TradingView when a separate multi-market chart and alert workspace is the missing job. Keep the exchange chart when it already covers your review and execution workflow.

## FAQ

### Is TradingView Basic enough for one crypto price alert?

Usually, yes. Basic lists three active price alerts, so one coin with a small number of price levels may fit. A user asking about continuous alerts for one coin raised this exact plan question in [this Reddit discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/TradingView/comments/1e2sk82/please_help_me_choose_the_right_trading_view/). Check whether the trigger you want is a price alert or a technical alert before upgrading.

### What TradingView plan do I need for trendline alerts?

The current pricing table lists no technical alerts on Basic and 20 on Essential. A user asking which subscription enables a trendline alert received the plan-capacity question in [this Reddit thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/TradingView/comments/1tkta4o/what_plan_subscription_do_we_need_to_use_alerts/). Count active technical alerts first; choose Essential within its limit and Plus above it.

### Do I need Premium for more crypto charts?

Not automatically. Premium provides eight charts per tab and 25 indicators per chart, but Plus has four charts and 10 indicators at a much lower regular annual price. During the current sale, Premium is only $1 more upfront than Plus; decide using the renewal cost and whether you will use the extra capacity.

### Do I have to pay extra for realtime crypto exchange data?

TradingView says the cryptocurrency exchanges shown on its platform provide realtime data. Its paid plans do not include all separately priced stock and futures data, but those are different from the crypto exchange feeds described in the [official market data guide](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000471705-how-to-purchase-additional-market-data/?aff_id=1173587), checked November 26, 2026. Confirm the exact symbol's status before adding a data package.

### Does the sale price renew at the same amount?

No. TradingView's official renewal note says discounts apply for the first 12 months only and annual subscriptions renew at regular rates. Use the 24 and 36 month totals above for planning, then confirm the amount shown to your account before checkout.

### Can a TradingView alert place an exchange order by itself?

No. A webhook can send a notification to an external receiver, which must be configured separately to connect to any execution service. Check security, duplicate handling and failure behavior before connecting alerts to live orders.
