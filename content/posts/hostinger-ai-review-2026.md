Hostinger's AI-assisted website tools are candidates for a client site when the editing workflow, ownership and future changes fit the project. First identify which product and mode the client would actually use. An AI-generated first version is only one part of delivery.

**Scope, October 6, 2026:** This is a documentation-based buying guide. We have not completed a hands-on build, export test or comparative reliability benchmark. Earlier numerical ratings, unsupported reviewer conclusions and fixed promotional prices have been removed.

**Links:** Hostinger links below are ordinary vendor sources. We have not verified ownership or approval of the previously used referral code. Related guides may contain separately disclosed affiliate links. [Disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).

## Identify the product before comparing offers

Hostinger documents [AI Website Builder for WordPress](https://www.hostinger.com/support/how-to-use-hostinger-ai-website-builder-for-wordpress/) separately from [AI Builder content export in manual mode](https://www.hostinger.com/support/6572573-hostinger-ai-builder-manual-mode-how-to-export-content-to-wordpress/). Both sources were checked October 6.

Write down the product, mode, plan and intended site. Do not transfer a feature, export permission or allowance from one offer to another merely because both use the Hostinger name. Verify the particular workflow in the documentation and account applicable to your purchase.

## WordPress content export is not a complete site migration

The manual-mode export guide describes transferring regular pages, text, images and other supported files. It excludes styles and layout, the online store, integrations, forms, collected submissions and SEO settings. Those limits belong to the documented manual-mode workflow; they do not establish what another product or mode supports.

Before promising a migration, list the elements that need rebuilding and the data that needs a separate export. A successful content transfer alone would not prove that the destination looks or behaves like the original site. We have not performed this export.

Do not change a live client's site simply to experiment. Use an authorized sample and follow the current vendor procedure; account access and any temporary connection should be reviewed by the owner.

## Test a representative client task

Evaluate the changes a client is likely to request after the first version: revise a service description, replace an image, change navigation and update a contact detail. Record the time, rejected suggestions and unresolved problems. Generated text and images still need factual and rights review.

| Client requirement | Observation to record |
|---|---|
| Editing | Can the client complete their common changes in the chosen mode? |
| Layout | Does the site remain usable at the required screen sizes? |
| Forms | Where do submissions go, and what access is required? |
| Portability | What content or settings can leave the platform, and what must be rebuilt? |
| Ownership | Who owns the hosting, domain and ongoing billing? |
| Maintenance | Who handles updates, recovery and support escalation? |

Leave the observations unmeasured until a real sample has been reviewed. A vendor demonstration is not our benchmark, and this article does not assign a numerical winner.

## Budget for the complete commitment

Check the current checkout for your country, currency and billing period. Record upfront payment, renewal, required add-ons, domain renewal and cancellation terms. Do not assume a displayed monthly equivalent is a monthly payment or that a domain is permanently free.

Include the time to review generated content, rebuild excluded export elements and train the client. Use our [project cost guide](/posts/freelance-tool-project-cost/) to separate cash due now from costs allocated to a project.

## Agree on handoff before buying

Record ownership, the client's editing needs and continuing support in the [blank WordPress handoff worksheet](/downloads/wordpress-handoff-worksheet.txt). Do not store passwords or tokens in that worksheet. Our [managed WordPress versus self-managed VPS guide](/posts/managed-wordpress-vps-hosting-2026/) addresses hosting responsibilities; it is a different decision from selecting a website editor.

Shortlist the specific builder when an authorized sample fits the client's work and its export limitations are acceptable. Resolve unanswered editing, portability and billing questions before committing. This guide does not prove that Hostinger is the cheapest, fastest or most reliable option.
