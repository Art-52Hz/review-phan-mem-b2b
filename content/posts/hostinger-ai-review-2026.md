## Core Features: What Actually Matters

### The AI Website Builder (The Real One, Not Horizons)

Hostinger's standard AI website builder is legitimately useful. You answer a few questions about your business or project, pick a style direction, and it generates a complete site structure with placeholder content and a coherent layout. For freelancers who bill by the hour, this is time saved — not just a gimmick.

The builder is closer in design flexibility to Squarespace than to a drag-and-drop toy. You can customize layouts with a grid-based editor, swap sections, and update content without touching code. It's not perfect — if you want pixel-level control or a heavily custom theme, you'll want WordPress instead — but for a clean professional result in 2026, the AI builder does what it promises.

One critical distinction: this review is talking about the **Website Builder's AI features**, not Hostinger Horizons. Those are two different products and they perform very differently. More on Horizons in the cons section.

### hPanel: Actually Clean, Occasionally Slow

hPanel is Hostinger's proprietary management dashboard, and it's genuinely one of the better control panels in the budget hosting space. Everything you need — domain management, email setup, SSL installation, file manager, database management — is organized clearly without the visual chaos of old-school cPanel interfaces.

One-click WordPress installation works exactly as advertised. You click, fill in two fields, and WordPress is live. For a freelancer managing multiple client sites, this is the kind of frictionless workflow that matters.

The one legitimate complaint: hPanel can load slowly at times. This isn't constant, but it has been flagged by multiple users. It's a minor irritant, not a dealbreaker — but it's worth knowing before you commit.

### WordPress Hosting That Actually Earns It

Hostinger is listed on WordPress.org's official recommended hosting providers page. That's not a marketing claim — that's WordPress itself endorsing Hostinger as a legitimate option. For freelancers building on WordPress (which, in 2026, is still the majority of the professional web), this matters.

The Business plan's NVMe storage is the technical reason behind the speed advantage. NVMe reads and writes faster than traditional SSD, which means faster page loads, better Core Web Vitals scores, and less time watching the loading spinner. For affiliate sites and blogs where SEO performance is tied directly to income, this is a real differentiator at the price point.

### VPS Hosting for Power Users

This is where Hostinger AI surprises people who only think of it as a beginner platform. The KVM VPS options are genuinely capable machines. The KVM 2 plan — the most popular VPS option — gives you 2 vCPU, 8GB RAM, 100GB NVMe storage, 8TB bandwidth, and a 1 Gbps network connection. The hardware runs on AMD EPYC processors, which is enterprise-grade silicon at a non-enterprise price.

VPS plans support Docker deployment, n8n automation workflows, and other developer templates out of the box. If you're a freelancer who's graduated from shared hosting and needs more control over your stack, this is a legitimate option worth considering — not just an upsell.

## FAQ

**1. Is the $2.69/month price for Hostinger AI real?**

Yes — but it's an introductory promotional price available on 48-month billing terms. When your first billing cycle ends, the Business plan renews at approximately $16.99/month. Always calculate the renewal price before committing, not just the headline rate.

**2. Is Hostinger AI good for affiliate marketing in 2026?**

Yes, it's one of the better-value options for affiliate bloggers. The Business plan's NVMe storage gives you speed for SEO, one-click WordPress installation is seamless, and managing multiple niche sites on one plan is cost-effective. Just plan for the renewal pricing.

**3. Can I use Hostinger AI to build an online store?**

For a small catalog or hobby store, yes. For a serious e-commerce operation that needs to scale, no. Both independent reviewers explicitly recommend Shopify or WooCommerce over Hostinger's native website builder for real online stores. This is not a dismissal — it's an honest segmentation of what the product does well.

**4. What is Hostinger Horizons and should I use it?**

Hostinger Horizons is a separate AI web application builder — different from the standard AI website builder. Based on reviewer feedback, it is experimental and unreliable in 2026. Small edits can trigger full site rebuilds that break existing elements. For AI-generated web apps, reviewers specifically recommend Base44 as a more stable alternative. Avoid Horizons for anything production-critical.

**5. Does Hostinger AI include a free domain?**

Yes, a free domain is typically included — but for the first year only. After that, you'll pay standard domain renewal fees. This is common across most hosts, but it's worth factoring into your first-year vs. ongoing cost comparison.
