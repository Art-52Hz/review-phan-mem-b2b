## Count the lookups before choosing

The daily caps are separate. One KWFinder search spends one keyword lookup and one SERP lookup because the search loads its first result and Keyword Difficulty. Selecting more keywords in the same result list spends more SERP lookups. Opening Autocomplete or Questions for the first time on that search spends another keyword lookup each. Counters reset 24 hours after the first lookup of that type, not at midnight.

For example, a day's 20 seed searches and 80 selected results can use 100 SERP lookups even though the keyword counter has room for more searches. If client work often needs that many result checks in one day, Premium's larger cap has a specific use. If not, Basic leaves $180 per year in your budget versus Premium on annual billing. This is a capacity comparison, not a claim that either plan produces better rankings.

Source: [Mangools lookup counting and reset rules](https://help.mangools.com/en/articles/17450713-how-lookups-and-limits-work-and-when-they-reset#a5d5c1d50feebf852f6d6f462), checked November 24, 2026. ## FAQ: price, limits and renewals

These questions reflect recurring buyer concerns in SEO forums, including whether an annual tool is worth keeping for sporadic work and how much the price has risen [in one Mangools pricing discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/SEO/comments/1pq9oh7/what_seo_software_should_i_use_now/).

### Is Mangools Entry still available?

Entry is absent from Mangools' current paid-plan comparison, which lists Basic, Premium and Agency. Do not budget an old Entry headline as a current offer; verify the plans shown in your account.

### Why can one KWFinder search use two lookups?

The search itself uses one keyword lookup, and loading its first result uses one SERP lookup. Selecting more results spends more SERP lookups. The two counters have separate daily limits.

### Should I pay annually if I use Mangools only for occasional projects?

Probably not at first. Monthly billing costs more per month but lets you stop after a short engagement. The annual Basic total is $452.40 upfront; the monthly total over 12 months is $732. Annual billing is a better fit when you expect to use the tool through most of the year.

### Do I need to buy the API, MCP or AI Search Watcher separately?

No separate purchase is listed for these in Basic or Premium. Mangools' plan comparison says SEO + AI plans include the SEO tools, API and MCP access, and AI Search Watcher Pro.

### Does canceling renewals refund the annual payment?

No. Turning off auto-renewal stops the next charge and keeps access until the paid period ends. Mangools offers a 48-hour full refund window on a first subscription; an unwanted annual auto-renewal may be refunded if requested within 14 days. Bank-transfer and cryptocurrency payments are non-refundable. Source: [Mangools refund rules](https://help.mangools.com/en/articles/17450724-refunds#a5d5c1d50feebf852f6d6f462), checked November 24, 2026.
