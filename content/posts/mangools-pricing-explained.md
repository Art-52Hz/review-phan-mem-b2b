## How lookups affect the plan you need

A keyword lookup and a SERP lookup are separate counters. A KWFinder search uses a keyword lookup and opens an initial result, consuming a SERP lookup too. Opening additional keywords consumes SERP capacity. Autocomplete and Questions can also consume additional keyword lookups. Reopening a keyword already opened in the same results does not consume another SERP lookup.

Each lookup counter resets 24 hours after its first use, rather than at midnight. Backlink rows reset monthly. Tracked keywords are occupied slots, not a daily allowance.

Source: [Mangools lookup counting and reset documentation](https://help.mangools.com/en/articles/17450713-how-lookups-and-limits-work-and-when-they-reset#a5d5c1d50feebf852f6d6f462), checked November 12, 2026.

**Planning example, not a measured session:** suppose you budget 20 seed searches and 80 additional keyword selections for one busy day. The initial results plus those selections could consume 100 SERP lookups while leaving much of the keyword-search allowance unused. A second brief that afternoon would need headroom. Count the work on your busiest day, rather than dividing a month's research evenly across a calendar.

That is the practical reason to consider Premium: fewer interruptions during paid delivery. A quieter freelancer can spread research across days and keep the smaller plan. Neither approach improves the quality of a brief unless you still inspect intent, competing pages and relevance to the client's offer.

## Who should choose which plan, and who should not

**Choose Basic for predictable solo delivery.** It is the sensible starting shortlist when you prepare article outlines and keyword briefs throughout the week. Upgrade after identifying a recurring capacity problem. Paying for spare rows before you have a client deliverable adds cost without improving the invoice.

**Choose Premium for concentrated research days or shared work.** Use a recent project to estimate how often you would hit the smaller plan's ceiling. Compare the extra subscription cost with the time lost waiting or rearranging deadlines. At the public annual examples above, the upgrade costs $180 more per year; use your own quote for the final decision.

**Consider Entry only if offered to you.** Ask which limits and renewal price apply. A cheap historical headline does not establish today's entitlement, and the current help comparison is a better starting point for a new purchase.

**Skip a paid plan if you are still defining the service.** New Mangools accounts receive Free+ for 10 days without a card, then move to Free. Both have five keyword and five SERP lookups per 24 hours. This is a limited way to inspect the workflow, not full Basic access. Source: [Mangools Free and Free+ documentation](https://help.mangools.com/en/articles/17450714-free-and-free-plans-explained#a5d5c1d50feebf852f6d6f462), checked November 12, 2026.

## How to test it yourself in 15 minutes

Use a small, real client topic on the free account before paying:

1. **Minutes 0-3:** write the audience, country, language and deliverable. Capture the remaining keyword and SERP counters.
2. **Minutes 3-7:** run a seed search. Note which ideas could support a useful client page.
3. **Minutes 7-11:** inspect a few relevant results within your remaining allowance. Record counter changes and why you kept or rejected each phrase.
4. **Minutes 11-15:** turn the shortlist into an outline. Estimate the searches required for the full brief and compare that with a busy day's schedule.

## FAQ: pricing, billing and upgrades

### Is Mangools Entry still available?

Its official launch article is still online, but the current paid-plan help comparison omits it. Check whether your account offers Entry before budgeting around it; this guide does not confirm present availability.

### Is annual billing a monthly installment plan?

No. The annual plan is paid for the whole year at once. The monthly equivalent is a comparison figure. Choose monthly billing when you need flexibility for a short engagement.

### Does Premium give unlimited keyword lookups?

No. Search allowances and suggestions per search are different. Premium increases research capacity, but its daily lookup counters still apply. Size the plan around actual searches and SERP checks.

### Can I upgrade later?

Yes. Mangools prorates upgrades using your existing plan and remaining paid time. Downgrades take effect immediately, with unused balance applied toward future renewals. Source: [Mangools upgrade and downgrade documentation](https://help.mangools.com/en/articles/17450716-upgrade-or-downgrade-your-plan#a5d5c1d50feebf852f6d6f462), checked November 12, 2026.

### Does canceling automatically refund the annual payment?

No. Stopping renewal preserves access through the paid period. Mangools documents a 48-hour first-subscription money-back guarantee and a separate 14-day request window for automatic annual renewals; business handling fees may apply. Bank-transfer and cryptocurrency payments are excluded from refunds. Source: [Mangools refund policy explanation](https://help.mangools.com/en/articles/17450724-refunds#a5d5c1d50feebf852f6d6f462), checked November 12, 2026.

Start with a real brief, choose the capacity it requires, and prepay only when the annual amount fits work you expect to keep doing.
