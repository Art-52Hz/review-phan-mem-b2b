## What Cryptocurrency to Use for VPS Payment

**Bitcoin (BTC):** Accepted everywhere, but transactions are on a public blockchain. Not truly anonymous without a mixer.

**USDT (Tether):** Stable value, wide acceptance at most crypto-friendly hosts. Practical for recurring payments.

**Monero (XMR):** Truly private by default — transactions are untraceable. Accepted at fewer providers but the gold standard for payment privacy.

**Ethereum (ETH):** Accepted at some providers, but high gas fees make small transactions impractical.

**Recommendation:** Use USDT for convenience and price stability. Use Monero if true anonymity is the priority.

## FAQ

**Is anonymous VPS actually anonymous?**
Payment anonymity is one layer. Complete anonymity also requires: registering with an anonymous email, using a VPN/Tor to access the control panel, and hosting in an offshore jurisdiction. UltaHost + Monero + ProtonMail + VPN = practical anonymous hosting setup.

**Do crypto-payment VPS providers offer refunds?**
Crypto transactions are irreversible. Most providers offer service credit rather than cash refunds. Read the refund policy before purchasing.

**Is offshore crypto VPS legal?**
Hosting itself is legal in offshore jurisdictions. What you host is your responsibility. Offshore hosting protects against unjustified DMCA requests — it doesn't protect against genuinely illegal content.
