A voice tool shortlist is useful only when its candidates address the job you need done. Generating narration, correcting recorded audio and listening to a document are different tasks. Start with the deliverable before comparing a subscription.

## Editing and other audio tasks

[Descript's AI-voice page](https://www.descript.com/ai-voices) describes stock voices, voice cloning and speech correction inside its editing workflow. These are documented capabilities, not our observations of editing speed or output quality. If you only need a finished narration file, check whether the additional editing workflow helps your task.

[Speechify's voice-generator page](https://speechify.com/ai-voice-generator/) presents an audio-creation product. Confirm which offering you are evaluating; a personal reading subscription should not be assumed to include the same production or export rights.

## Build a shortlist you can defend

Write the language, required file format, intended distribution, project length and revision allowance. Eliminate candidates that cannot establish a mandatory requirement before scoring preferences such as tone.

Evaluate the same short script in each remaining candidate. Include names, numbers and specialist vocabulary. Keep settings, actual attempts and active revision time with each result. An unknown requirement should remain unknown rather than turning into a confident score.
