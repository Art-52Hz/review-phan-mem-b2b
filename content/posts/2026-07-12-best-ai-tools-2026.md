## Start with the job you need to complete

Write down one deliverable, the format the client needs, the deadline and the permission to use the input material. Choose a small shortlist against that job. A provider's demonstration is an input to your evaluation; it does not prove the output meets your client's requirements.

- For search planning, follow the [keyword research workflow](/posts/keyword-research-for-freelancers/). Record intent and evidence before selecting a topic.
- For narration work, use the [voiceover evaluation scorecard](/posts/voiceover-evaluation-scorecard/). Compare one script, revisions, export requirements and rights separately.
- For hosting, use the [VPS buying checklist](/posts/ultahost-vps-review/). Record renewal cost, backups, recovery and support scope before purchase.

## Evaluate the shortlist before subscribing

1. **Requirements:** Record the exact task and acceptance criteria. Do not use a general popularity score as a substitute for the output you need.
2. **Current plan:** Read the official pricing page at purchase time. Note currency, taxes, billing period, seat minimums, usage limits and any renewal or trial cancellation terms.
3. **Evidence:** Try the same representative input where access permits, record the date and keep your actual observations. Leave untested criteria blank rather than inventing scores.
4. **Rights and privacy:** Check the license, client consent, input-data policy and export requirements for your work. Do not upload confidential client files without authorization.
5. **Total project cost:** Separate the cash you commit from the portion allocated to one project. Include revision time and setup time using your own recorded figures.
6. **Decision:** Keep a reason for accepting or rejecting each candidate. A free plan or a larger feature list does not itself establish a good fit.

## What this page does not measure

This directory does not measure speed, accuracy, uptime, return on investment or commission performance. We have not established a single best tool for every freelancer. For a buying decision, use current vendor documentation and your own representative evaluation alongside the linked editorial material.
