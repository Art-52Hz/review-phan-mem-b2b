## What Is Murf AI?

## Key Features in 2026

### 120+ AI Voices — Studio Quality

Murf's voice library includes 120+ AI voices organized by:
- **Gender:** Male, female
- **Language:** 20+ languages including English US/UK/AU, Spanish, French, German, Hindi, and more
- **Style:** Conversational, professional, energetic, calm, authoritative
- **Age range:** Young adult, middle-aged, senior

**Real quality assessment:** Murf's English voices (especially US and UK) are genuinely difficult to distinguish from real voice actors in many contexts. The quality gap between Murf and a professional studio recording is narrow enough that most viewers won't notice.

Every voice can be previewed before generating — no need to produce a full script just to test a voice.

### Granular Voice Control

This is where Murf pulls ahead of many TTS competitors. You can fine-tune:

- **Pitch:** raise or lower the voice register (lower = deeper, more authoritative)
- **Speed:** 0.5x to 2x playback rate
- **Emphasis:** stress specific words — wrap in `*asterisks*` to emphasize
- **Pauses:** insert custom silences between sentences or sections
- **Pronunciation:** fix how technical terms, brand names, or acronyms are read

**Practical example:** Script contains "SEO" — by default the AI reads "Ess-ee-oh." You can override to read it exactly how you want. Small detail, but critical for tech and B2B content.

### Video Sync — Voiceover Without Leaving Murf

Murf has a built-in video editor: upload your video → add voiceover → adjust timing → export. No separate video editing software needed for basic narration.

This is a genuine workflow win for explainer videos, tutorials, and presentation recordings.

**Limitation:** The video editor is basic — timing sync and simple cuts only. For full editing (b-roll, transitions, captions), you still need Descript, Premiere, or DaVinci.

### Voice Changer

Record your own voice → Murf converts it to any AI voice in the library. Useful if you want to preserve your natural cadence and inflection while upgrading the audio quality, or if you want to maintain voice consistency across a project without re-recording.

### Collaboration Features

Share projects with editors or clients, leave comments on the audio timeline, and access version history. Available on Business and Enterprise plans — not on the Individual/Creator plan.
