## VGT: why finished audio can cost more than its runtime

Murf calculates Voice Generation Time (VGT) from the speech generated in each sub-block. Its guide estimates about 6 minutes of VGT for a 1,000-word English script, before text revisions. Editing text and generating that sub-block again uses VGT; changing voice, style, pitch, speed, pause, emphasis or pronunciation on the same text does not. Source: [Murf VGT guide](https://help.murf.ai/what-is-voice-generation-time-vgt), checked November 23, 2026.

This means a five-minute delivered video is not automatically a five-minute VGT job. Rewriting and re-rendering sections adds usage. Murf's published yearly allowances average to 2 hours a month on Creator and 8 hours a month on Business, but unused time is not unlimited: [Murf's VGT validity policy](https://help.murf.ai/vgt-validity), checked November 23, 2026, describes carry-forward rules, and [its cancellation article](https://help.murf.ai/what-happens-to-my-account-and-projects-after-canceling), checked November 23, 2026, says remaining plan and carried-forward VGT are lost after cancellation.

## Which one should you pick?

- **You publish a few client explainers each month and work alone:** Choose Creator if your trial sample passes and the client accepts the standard commercial rights. It costs $228 upfront for the year.
- **A registered company is buying or publishing the work:** Choose Business because Murf recommends its Business License for registered businesses. Put the license holder and permitted uses in the project agreement.
- **You narrate PowerPoint or Google Slides and revise frequently:** Choose Business only if its integrations, controls or 96-hour annual allowance solve a real production need; otherwise Creator is $564 less per year.
- **You need a single short voiceover and no repeat work is booked:** Do not commit to an annual plan yet. Use the trial to audition the voice, then compare an option with a billing term that matches the job.
- **Your audio will air on TV or commercial radio:** Ask Murf for an Enterprise quote. Murf reserves broadcasting rights for Enterprise; neither Creator nor Business is the right purchase for that distribution.

## Voice quality: audition the part that can fail

Voice quality depends on the chosen voice, language, script and delivery settings. There is no controlled Murf-versus-competitor listening result here, so a broad claim that Murf sounds more or less natural would be unsupported. The practical test is whether your selected voice handles the difficult passage in your actual project.

Use the free trial's 10 minutes to preview names, technical terms, numbers and the emotional line that matters most. Change settings on the same generated text to compare delivery without using more VGT, according to Murf's guide. ## Commercial rights and limits

Murf says all paid Studio plans provide commercial rights for generated voiceovers, including original content on YouTube and other streaming platforms. It recommends the Business License for registered businesses. Broadcasting rights for TV networks and commercial radio are Enterprise-only. Source: [Murf commercial rights guidance](https://help.murf.ai/do-i-have-commercial-rights-over-the-voice-over-created), checked November 23, 2026.

Murf also says commercial and Business License rights for voiceovers already created remain valid after a subscription ends. Source: [Murf post-subscription rights FAQ](https://help.murf.ai/will-my-commercial-rights-and-business-license-be-valid-after-my-subscription-has-ended), checked November 23, 2026. That does not preserve paid Studio access or unused VGT. Rights to the script, music and footage are separate, and publishing platforms may update their AI-content policies.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I download a Murf voiceover on the free trial?

No. Murf's trial allows previews and project sharing, but downloads are excluded. A paid plan is needed to export.

### Can I use Murf voices in monetized YouTube videos?

Murf says paid Studio plans include commercial rights for original YouTube and streaming content. The free trial does not grant those rights. Check YouTube's current AI disclosure rules for the upload.

### Does changing a voice or pronunciation use VGT?

Not when you keep the text unchanged. Murf says voice, style, pitch, speed, pause, emphasis and pronunciation changes on the same text do not consume VGT. Changing text and rendering its sub-block again does.

### Do my rights end when I cancel Murf?

Murf says rights for voiceovers you created remain valid after cancellation. Your paid features and downloads end with the billing cycle, and remaining VGT is lost.

### Is Murf worth paying for one video?

Usually only if the export and rights justify the full $228 Creator annual invoice. Preview the hardest passage in the trial first; if it passes and you have repeat narration work, Creator is the lower-cost paid plan. Otherwise avoid an annual commitment for a one-off.
