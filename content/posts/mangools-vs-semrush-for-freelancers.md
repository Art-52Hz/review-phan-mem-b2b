## Who should choose Mangools, and who should not?

Choose Mangools Basic if your recurring output is a short, well-reasoned keyword brief. It is a sensible shortlist when you work alone, research one topic at a time and can organize searches around a daily allowance. Start with the buyer's problem, collect candidate phrases, then inspect the results before assigning a page to each topic.

Basic's documented limits include 200 suggestions per search and up to 25 competitor keywords per domain search. Those constraints matter more for broad competitor discovery than for a narrow article brief. A researcher exploring a large client site should check whether that restricted competitor view leaves useful topics unseen before buying.

Premium raises keyword and SERP lookups to 500 each per 24 hours and includes three sub-users, but still has weekly rank updates. Upgrade for a demonstrated research or collaboration bottleneck, not because a larger plan looks more professional. If daily ranking reports are part of your contract, Basic and Premium are a poor fit for that particular promise.

## Who should choose Semrush, and who should not?

Choose Semrush when the brief is only one part of your paid responsibility. A retainer that includes technical recommendations, implementation follow-up and ongoing search monitoring gives a broader suite more opportunities to earn its cost. The entry SEO plan explicitly lists Site Audit and Position Tracking alongside research.

Avoid paying for that breadth if your assignment ends with a keyword spreadsheet and you already have a dependable way to produce it. Tool access is overhead until it changes what you can deliver, the time required or the quality of a decision. A bigger interface is not itself a reason to charge more.

Also count the client websites you must monitor simultaneously. A plan that fits the first client may become awkward when you add another active engagement. Decide in advance which projects must remain monitored between reporting dates rather than buying capacity based only on your current research task.

Semrush's pricing page lists additional users from $45/month and reporting add-ons separately. If a collaborator or branded report is essential, include the relevant extras in your quote. Do not assume every capability advertised across the platform is bundled into its entry price.

## Which keyword tool pays off?

There is no defensible universal earnings figure. Use your own delivered work and a simple break-even calculation:

**Required useful hours saved per month = subscription cost per month / your contribution per working hour.**

Contribution means what remains available to cover overhead after direct delivery costs. Use your own figure, not an industry salary estimate. Time saved counts only if it helps you finish profitable work or avoids a real expense; an unused afternoon does not automatically become revenue.

For Semrush, put the verified $139 monthly subscription into that calculation. For Mangools, enter the monthly amount shown for your location. Add paid seats and other necessary tools before deciding. Keep annual upfront cash separate from the monthly equivalent: a lower equivalent can still be the wrong purchase when your workload is uncertain.

Compare the two subscriptions on the same billing basis. Then calculate the extra cost of the broader option and ask which specific deliverable covers it. If the answer is a technical audit you already sell, the extra capability may be valuable. If the answer is merely that you might offer audits someday, begin with the smaller commitment.

## How to test it yourself in 15 minutes

Treat this as an initial fit check using available free access, not a complete evaluation of paid capacity. Use the same client topic and target market in each tool so the comparison answers a practical question.

1. **Minutes 0-3: define the output.** Write down the audience, country, language and page you need to produce. Decide what would make a phrase commercially relevant before looking at volume.
2. **Minutes 3-7: collect a small shortlist.** Search the same seed topic in each available interface. Record candidate phrases, date, location and any displayed limits. If free access blocks an action, record that limit rather than extrapolating a result.
3. **Minutes 7-11: inspect intent.** Review the results for your strongest candidate. Are the ranking pages product pages, comparisons, tutorials or local services? Reject a phrase if your planned page cannot satisfy the apparent need.
4. **Minutes 11-13: prepare the handoff.** Export if your access allows it, or manually record the shortlist. Include the recommended page type and a short reason for each choice. Check whether the resulting document is understandable without a tool login.
5. **Minutes 13-15: record friction.** Note unclear settings, restrictions and unfinished steps. Pick the workflow that helped you make a justified decision, then test it on a complete real brief before committing annually.

## FAQ

### Can I use either tool for free?

Yes. Mangools advertises a free account, and Semrush publishes a limited free option in its pricing comparison. Begin there to check the workflow. Free access is not evidence that the paid plan can handle your entire client workload.

### Is Mangools enough for freelance keyword research?

It is a reasonable shortlist for focused briefs if its research limits fit your busiest day. For a broad competitor inventory, check the restricted domain-search results before choosing Basic. For a contract requiring daily rank updates, choose a plan with that cadence explicitly included.

### Which Semrush plan should a solo freelancer compare first?

Start the comparison with the SEO plan if keyword research and website SEO are the tasks you sell. Assess Starter separately if an AI search deliverable is part of the contract. Write down the required output before paying for the bundle.

### Should I choose monthly or annual billing?

Choose monthly while proving that the subscription belongs in your workflow. Consider annual billing once you have recurring work and know the required limits. Before payment, record the full charge, renewal date and applicable refund conditions; cancellation and refund eligibility are separate questions.

### Do I need both Mangools and Semrush?

Usually, one primary research subscription is the more disciplined starting point. Add the second only when you can name a repeated task the first does not cover well and show that the additional cost is justified. Duplicate keyword lists alone are a weak reason to maintain two bills.
