An unlimited bandwidth label does not establish that every workload is permitted or that the connection has unlimited speed. Before choosing a plan, record the transfer allowance, port speed, fair-use rules and action the provider may take when usage exceeds its permitted scope.

## Read the policy alongside the plan

UltaHost's [Unlimited Bandwidth Policy](https://ultahost.com/unlimited-bandwidth-policy), read October 7, 2026, describes no fixed monthly transfer cap on selected plans, subject to acceptable use and effects on other users. Its listed enforcement actions include temporary throttling, suspension and termination for repeated violations. It restricts public file-sharing and streaming services and use as a CDN substitute. Some restrictions are explicitly specific to shared hosting; ask which conditions apply to your exact VPS order.

That policy does not support the old claim that throttling can never occur. Obtain a dated answer for the proposed workload before purchase; a general headline is insufficient acceptance evidence.

## Describe the workload before comparing quotes

| Requirement | Record before ordering |
|---|---|
| Transfer volume | Expected monthly transfer and peak traffic, with the basis of the estimate |
| Network throughput | Included port speed and whether any rate is shared or guaranteed |
| Content delivery | File types, download sizes and whether the workload is allowed |
| Fair use | Applicable policy, warning process and possible service actions |
| Operations | Person responsible for monitoring usage and responding to an incident |
| Full cost | Billing term, renewal quote, taxes, add-ons and upgrade authorization |

Separate monthly data volume from the rate at which data can move. A traffic-heavy download service and a small client WordPress site can have different requirements even when both ask for unlimited bandwidth. Do not assume a higher CPU allocation resolves a network or policy constraint.

Record the renewal quote and cancellation deadline separately from any introductory price. The previous statement that renewal always equals the introductory rate has been removed.

## Make a permitted acceptance plan

Before ordering, write down the application, region, workload and checks the client expects. Measure only in an authorized environment. Record observed results and unresolved issues; do not promise a latency, uptime credit or security certification without applicable evidence.
