## How to test it yourself in 15 minutes

Use this as an initial acceptance exercise after obtaining an environment you are authorized to use. It is not a claim that a short check proves long-term reliability.

1. List the application, operating system, database and panel you intend to run. Compare their requirements with the selected plan before installation.
2. Check access to the server and its management panel. Record who owns the account and who is responsible for updates.
3. Deploy a small copy of your actual workload with sample data. Confirm the main page, scheduled job or API action works.
4. Inspect resource usage while completing one representative task. Record the configuration and observation rather than guessing future capacity.
5. Locate the backup and restore workflow. Restore a disposable copy before planning a production migration.
6. Save the invoice details and support scope with your project handoff. Schedule a longer evaluation under realistic traffic before switching a client.
