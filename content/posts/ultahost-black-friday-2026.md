## Which one should you pick?

- **A small Linux site or service with requirements below 1 GB RAM:** choose VPS Basic on 24 months if you have validated the software and want the smaller upfront commitment. Choose 36 months only when your three-year need is credible.
- **A client app that needs around 2 GB RAM:** choose VPS Business if its 2 cores, 2 GB RAM and 50 GB NVMe match the app's documented requirements. Add the full renewal estimate to the project budget.
- **A database or multi-service setup that exceeds Business:** shortlist Professional or Enterprise only after checking the actual memory and storage requirement. A sale does not make spare capacity free.
- **A freelancer who can administer Linux and wants more RAM per dollar:** compare Hostinger before buying; start with our [UltaHost vs Hostinger VPS comparison](/posts/ultahost-vs-hostinger-vps-2026/).
- **A project still in a trial or migration stage:** choose monthly billing or wait until the application and recovery plan are ready. Do not use a three-year prepaid plan as a test environment.
