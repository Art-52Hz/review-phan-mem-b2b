## Instant and Professional have different input limits

Instant Voice Cloning uses a short reference recording and is available on Starter. ElevenLabs recommends about 1 to 2 minutes of clean audio and says more than 3 minutes can add little or harm the result. Record one speaker in a consistent tone without room echo, music or background noise. Source: [Instant Voice Cloning recording guidance](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-creative/voices/voice-cloning/instant-voice-cloning), checked Nov 27, 2026.

Professional Voice Cloning trains a dedicated model and requires Creator or above. The current upload guidance recommends 30 to 180 minutes of good audio. Creator supplies one PVC slot, and the voice owner must pass verification. Training time varies; ElevenLabs gives a typical fine-tuning estimate of 3 to 6 hours, which is not a delivery guarantee. Sources: [Professional Voice Cloning overview](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-creative/voices/voice-cloning), [file and recording requirements](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/product/voices/voice-cloning/what-files-do-you-accept-for-voice-cloning), checked Nov 27, 2026.

Credits are shared across ElevenLabs products. A monthly credit allowance is not a fixed number of finished minutes: the selected model and text length determine usage, and retries draw from the pool too. If Professional cloning is the only reason to upgrade, Creator is the entry plan with that feature; Pro is not required.

- **You want to hear your own voice on a short sample before deciding:** pick Free to explore available voices, then use Starter for an Instant clone. Free has no cloning entitlement, and Free output is not licensed for commercial use.

## Consent, verification and commercial use

Instant Voice Cloning requires you to confirm that you have the right and consent to clone the voice. Professional Voice Cloning is stricter: ElevenLabs says you can create a PVC only of your own voice, even if someone else consents. The speaker can verify their own clone and share it privately with you. Sources: [Instant cloning consent step](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-creative/voices/voice-cloning/instant-voice-cloning) and [Professional clone ownership rule](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/product/voices/voice-cloning/can-i-create-a-professional-voice-clone-of-someone-elses-voice), checked Nov 27, 2026.

Paid plans include a commercial license for eligible output, provided you hold the necessary rights and follow the service terms. Free-plan output cannot be used commercially, and a later subscription does not license an earlier Free generation. Beta services are excluded from commercial use. Keep the speaker's authorization, permitted project scope and final deliverables together. Source: [official publishing and commercial-use policy](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/legal/can-i-publish-the-content-i-generate-on-the-platform), checked Nov 27, 2026.

If you downgrade below Creator, the Professional clone remains in the account but cannot be used until you return to Creator or above. Output already created under a paid plan may remain commercially usable under the policy, but future changes require access to the clone again. Source: [official downgrade guidance](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/administration/account), checked Nov 27, 2026.

## FAQ

### Is voice cloning free on ElevenLabs?

No. Free includes 10,000 monthly credits for evaluation but no voice cloning entitlement or commercial license. Starter adds Instant Voice Cloning; Creator adds Professional Voice Cloning. Source: [official pricing page](https://elevenlabs.io/pricing), checked Nov 27, 2026.

### Can I create a Professional clone of a client if they sign a consent form?

No. ElevenLabs permits a Professional clone only of your own voice, even with another person's consent. The client must make and verify the PVC in their own account, then share it with you. Source: [official PVC ownership rule](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/product/voices/voice-cloning/can-i-create-a-professional-voice-clone-of-someone-elses-voice), checked Nov 27, 2026.

### How much audio should I prepare for Instant cloning?

Aim for 1 to 2 minutes of clear, consistent speech. The current guide says more than 3 minutes may add little or reduce quality. Source: [Instant Voice Cloning guide](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-creative/voices/voice-cloning/instant-voice-cloning), checked Nov 27, 2026.

### How many Professional clones come with Creator?

One PVC slot. The documented base limit is one each for Creator and Pro; Scale and Business have more. Source: [official PVC slot limits](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/product/voices/voice-cloning/how-many-professional-voice-clones-pv-cs-can-i-have), checked Nov 27, 2026.

### If I cancel, can I still use my clone?

The clone is not deleted, but it is locked when the account drops below the plan required for that clone. A recent user question on [Reddit about clone access after cancellation](https://www.reddit.com/r/ElevenLabs/comments/1wh27p9/can_i_keep_using_my_cloned_voice_after_the/) reflects this common concern. Download and check all deliverables before your plan changes; source: [official downgrade guidance](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/administration/account), checked Nov 27, 2026.

### Can I use Free audio commercially after upgrading?

No. The commercial license applies to eligible content generated during a paid subscription. Upgrading later does not make earlier Free output commercial. Source: [official publishing policy](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/legal/can-i-publish-the-content-i-generate-on-the-platform), checked Nov 27, 2026.

Choose Starter for a first Instant clone, and Creator only when a dedicated Professional model or its credit allowance supports recurring work. Price the renewal before subscribing, and make sure the speaker completes the right verification and sharing steps.
