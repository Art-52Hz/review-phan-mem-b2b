## Compare a complete quote

| Quote item | What to obtain |
|---|---|
| Hardware | Exact model, memory and storage configuration for the order |
| Network | Port rate, transfer allowance and applicable fair-use rules |
| Management | Tasks the provider handles and tasks left to your operator |
| Recovery | Backup destination, retention, restore process and cost |
| Billing | Initial term, renewal amount, setup fees, licenses and taxes |
| Change | Upgrade process, downtime expectations and cost approver |
| Incident | Contact route, support scope and any applicable service-credit conditions |

Make monthly and prepaid quotes separate rows. Include maintenance labour and external services in your project estimate rather than treating the server bill as the entire operating cost.
