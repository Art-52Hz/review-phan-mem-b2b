## What Is Loom?

Loom is an **async video messaging** tool. The core idea: replace the back-and-forth of long emails, drawn-out chat threads, and unnecessary meetings with a short, personal video. You record your screen (and optionally your webcam), and Loom instantly generates a link you can drop anywhere — Slack, email, a ticket, a doc.

What makes it "async" is that nobody has to be present. You record on your schedule; they watch on theirs. For distributed teams across time zones, that's a quiet superpower: you keep the clarity and warmth of seeing a face and hearing a voice, without the calendar tetris of a live call.

The magic is the speed. From "I should explain this" to "here's the link" takes under a minute, and there's zero editing required for it to be useful.

## Key Features

### Screen + Camera Recording

The foundation: record your screen, your camera, or both at once (a "bubble" of your webcam in the corner). You can capture a full screen, a single window, or a browser tab, and use simple on-screen tools like a cursor highlighter and drawing. The result feels personal — viewers see your face and hear your tone, which carries nuance that text can't.

### AI Transcripts (Automatic)

Every Loom video gets an **automatic transcript**, generated without you lifting a finger. That makes videos searchable, accessible, and skimmable — viewers can read along, jump to a moment, or grab a quote. The transcription quality in 2026 is strong across accents and technical vocabulary.

### AI Summaries

Loom AI can generate a concise **summary** of any recording, so a busy recipient can read the gist in seconds and decide whether to watch the whole thing. It can also auto-title videos and pull out key points — a small touch that meaningfully improves how digestible your videos are.

### Filler-Word Removal

One of the most loved AI features: Loom can automatically detect and **remove filler words** ("um," "uh," "like") and even awkward pauses. The result is a tighter, more professional video without you having to open an editor. It's the closest thing to a "make me sound polished" button.

### Viewer Engagement Analytics

Loom shows you **who watched, how much they watched, and where they reacted or commented**. For sales, support, and client work, knowing a prospect watched your whole demo (or dropped off at the pricing slide) is genuinely actionable intelligence you don't get from a sent email.

### Loom AI (Chapters & Task Detection)

The deeper AI layer can automatically generate **chapters** for longer videos so viewers can navigate, and **detect action items / tasks** mentioned in the recording — turning a spoken walkthrough into a clear list of next steps. This is where Loom moves from "recorder" to "smart communication tool."
