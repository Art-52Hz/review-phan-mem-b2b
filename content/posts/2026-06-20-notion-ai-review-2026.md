Before paying for Notion AI, identify one client workflow you want to improve and check which plan actually supplies the required capabilities. An attractive demonstration does not establish how much revision, setup or checking your own work will need.

**Review scope:** This is a source-based buying checklist, not a hands-on benchmark. We have not established three months of product testing or a measured time-saving result. Vendor documentation was checked on October 3, 2026.

## Check current plan access first

Notion's [AI help page](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-ai-faqs) says AI is available on Business and Enterprise, while Free and Plus have limited complimentary responses. Business and Enterprise also have usage allowances for certain features; premium models can spend credits after an administrator enables them. Do not assume every AI action is unlimited.

The [pricing page](https://www.notion.com/pricing) lists AI trials for Free and Plus, and AI capabilities within Business. Custom Agents have separate Notion-credit requirements. This differs from treating AI as a universal $10 monthly add-on for every workspace.

Open the current plan comparison and your workspace billing screen before deciding. Record currency, seats, billing period, payment due now, renewal terms and any credit spending that the proposed workflow requires. A yearly price expressed per month is different from the cash commitment due at checkout.

[Check current Notion plans](https://www.notion.com/pricing). This is a plain vendor link; we have not verified a Notion affiliate relationship for this project.

## Choose one task to evaluate

Notion documents workspace search, writing assistance, meeting notes and agents that can create or edit pages and databases. Connected-app availability and access depend on your setup. Those documented capabilities are not measurements of output accuracy or a promise that your client workflow will succeed. [Source: Notion AI help](https://www.notion.com/help/notion-ai-faqs).

Start with a bounded task using non-sensitive sample material. A useful freelancer example is turning an approved project brief into a draft handoff note. Keep the original brief, the expected deliverable and your acceptance criteria together so another reviewer can understand the comparison.

Write down what a good result must contain before generating anything: the correct deadline, agreed scope, unresolved questions and the source of each important statement. Include one item that is deliberately absent from the input. The output should leave it unresolved rather than inventing an answer.

## Record the result instead of scoring the demo

Use this small evaluation record for each attempt:

| Check | Record |
|---|---|
| Input and scope | Sample brief and task requested |
| Plan eligibility | Feature available, unavailable or still unknown |
| Source accuracy | Important statements checked against the input |
| Missing information | Whether unknowns stay clearly unresolved |
| Editing work | Active minutes spent checking and revising |
| Delivery | Final file or page opened by the intended recipient |
| Cost | Billing commitment, allocated usage and required credits |

An answer that looks polished can still fail the source or delivery checks. Keep those as separate gates. If you change the prompt or sample halfway through, note the change rather than presenting the attempts as a controlled comparison.

## Compare with the workflow you already use

Run the same task manually using the same brief and acceptance criteria. Record active working time for both methods, including setup, review and handoff. One short sample is a starting observation; it does not support a site-wide claim that a product saves a fixed percentage of time.

If your existing workflow already meets the requirement, an upgrade needs a specific benefit worth its additional commitment. If the AI workflow fails a required check, more attractive writing does not compensate for the missing deliverable.

For a project-level estimate, separate subscription allocation, add-ons and your chosen value of active working time. Label estimates clearly and keep cash payments distinct from hypothetical savings. Our [presentation export checklist](/posts/beautiful-ai-review-2026/) covers a separate client deliverable; use it when slide editing and handoff are part of your project.

## Decide from your own evidence

Keep the plan details and observation date beside the evaluation. Repeat the sample when scope, permissions or billing change. Upgrade only after the required capabilities, acceptable output and complete cost are clear enough for your project decision.

If your task is SEO planning rather than workspace organization, start with the [keyword research workflow](/posts/keyword-research-for-freelancers/). Choosing a task first makes it easier to compare the relevant tool instead of buying a general AI feature without a concrete use.
