## Key Features

### Keyword Research — The Core Strength
Semrush's keyword database covers 25+ billion keywords across 142 geographic databases. You type a keyword, and you get search volume, keyword difficulty, CPC, trend data, related keywords, questions, and a full breakdown of what's ranking. For content strategy, it's the most comprehensive single source available.

The **Keyword Magic Tool** is the standout: you enter a seed keyword and it generates hundreds of related terms grouped by topic cluster — perfect for building a complete content calendar in one session.

### Competitor Intelligence
Enter any domain and see their top organic keywords, estimated traffic, backlink profile, paid ad keywords, and display advertising. For freelancers doing competitive research for clients, this is extremely valuable. You can find content gaps — topics your client's competitors rank for that they don't.

### Backlink Analysis
Semrush's backlink database is among the largest available, indexing over 43 trillion backlinks. You can audit your backlink profile, identify toxic links, find link-building opportunities, and track competitors' link acquisition over time.

### Site Audit
Technical SEO crawling with prioritized issue reports. It flags broken links, duplicate content, missing meta tags, page speed issues, and Core Web Vitals problems. The audit runs automatically on a schedule, so you catch new issues without remembering to check.

### Position Tracking
Track keyword rankings across any location (country, city, zip code) and device type. Essential for client reporting — you can show exactly how rankings changed over time with clear visualizations.

### Semrush AI (2026 Addition)
Semrush has integrated AI across several features: AI-generated content briefs, AI writing in the SEO Content Template, and AI-powered competitor summaries. The AI doesn't replace specialized writing tools, but it adds useful automation to research workflows.

### Semrush Academy
Free courses and certifications in SEO, content marketing, analytics, and Semrush-specific workflows. One of the most underrated parts of the platform — useful for onboarding clients or upskilling yourself.

## FAQ

**Is there a free version of Semrush?**
No permanent free plan. Semrush offers a 14-day free trial and a limited free account with restricted daily searches. For meaningful use, a paid plan is required.

**How does Semrush compare to Ahrefs?**
Semrush wins on competitor intelligence, content tools, and breadth. Ahrefs wins on backlink data accuracy and link research. Both are industry-leading; the choice comes down to which workflows matter more to you.

**Can I use Semrush for one month and cancel?**
Yes. Month-to-month billing is available. If you're running a one-time audit or doing a focused keyword research session, a single month at $140 is often worth it before canceling.

**Is Semrush good for beginners?**
It can be — Semrush Academy and the platform's built-in tutorials help. But beginners often find the scope overwhelming. Start with the Keyword Magic Tool and Position Tracking, then explore other features as needed.
