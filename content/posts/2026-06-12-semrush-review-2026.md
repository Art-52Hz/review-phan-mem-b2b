## Semrush Features in 2026

### 1. Keyword Research — The Core Strength

Semrush's keyword database covers **25+ billion keywords** across 142 geographic databases. Enter a keyword and get: search volume, keyword difficulty, CPC, SERP features, related keywords, questions, and trend data.

The **Keyword Magic Tool** is the standout feature. Type a seed keyword and get hundreds of related terms grouped by topic cluster — perfect for building a complete content calendar in a single session. The "Questions" filter alone saves hours of research: it surfaces real user questions from Reddit, Quora, and People Also Ask.

**In practice:** For "best VPN for freelancers", Semrush returns 847 related keywords sorted by difficulty, plus 120+ question variants. That's a month of content ideas from one search.

### 2. Competitor Intelligence — Unmatched

Enter any domain and get:
- Top organic keywords driving their traffic
- Estimated monthly visits
- Full backlink profile
- Paid ad keywords and spend estimates
- Content gaps: topics competitors rank for that you don't

For freelancers doing competitive research for clients, this is the single most valuable feature. You can walk into a client meeting and show exactly why their competitor is outranking them — and what content to create to close the gap.

### 3. Backlink Analysis

Semrush indexes over **43 trillion backlinks**. You can:
- Audit your full backlink profile
- Identify and disavow toxic links
- Find link-building opportunities by analyzing competitor backlinks
- Track new and lost links over time

The Backlink Gap tool is particularly useful: compare your profile against 4 competitors and see which sites link to all of them but not you — instant link prospecting list.

### 4. Site Audit

Technical SEO crawling with prioritized issue reports. Semrush crawls your site, then flags:
- Broken links (internal and external)
- Duplicate content and thin pages
- Missing or duplicate meta tags
- Page speed and Core Web Vitals issues
- HTTPS and security problems

The audit runs automatically on a schedule, so you catch new issues without checking manually. For agency work, this is extremely useful — you can set up automated weekly audits for each client and get notified by email.

### 5. Position Tracking

Track keyword rankings across any location (country, state, city, zip code) and device type. Essential for client reporting — you can show exactly how rankings changed over time with clear visualizations.

The **Daily Rankings** feature (Guru+) updates positions every 24 hours, which matters when you're tracking competitive or volatile keywords.

### 6. Semrush AI — 2026 Update

Semrush has significantly expanded its AI capabilities this year:

- **AI Content Brief Generator**: Input a keyword, get a structured brief with suggested headings, questions to answer, word count targets, and competitor content analysis
- **AI Writing in SEO Content Template**: Drafts sections directly in the editor with on-page SEO optimization built in
- **AI Competitor Summaries**: One-click summaries of any competitor's SEO strategy
- **Copilot (Beta)**: An AI assistant that proactively surfaces insights — "Your competitor gained 2,400 new backlinks this week, 340 from news sites"

The AI features don't replace specialized writing tools like Jasper or Copy.ai, but they add useful automation to research and briefing workflows.

### 7. Local SEO Toolkit

New in 2025-2026: Semrush's local SEO suite handles listing management, Google Business Profile optimization, and local rank tracking across multiple locations. Useful for agencies managing local clients.

### 8. Semrush Academy

Free courses and certifications in SEO, content marketing, PPC, and analytics. Particularly useful for:
- Onboarding new team members
- Providing client education
- Building your own expertise in areas adjacent to your core service

Over 40 courses are available, and the certifications are recognized by employers.

## FAQ

**Is there a free version of Semrush?**
No permanent free plan. Semrush offers a 7-day free trial and a limited free account with restricted daily searches (10 keyword lookups, 10 domain analyses per day). For meaningful use, a paid plan is required.

**How does Semrush compare to Ahrefs in 2026?**
Semrush wins on competitor intelligence, content tools, AI features, and breadth. Ahrefs wins on backlink data accuracy and link research. Both are industry-leading — the choice comes down to your primary workflows.

**Can I use Semrush for one month and cancel?**
Yes. Month-to-month billing is available. If you're running a one-time audit or focused keyword research sprint, a single month at $140 is often worth it before canceling.

**Is Semrush good for beginners?**
It can be — Semrush Academy and in-product tutorials help. Start with the Keyword Magic Tool and Position Tracking, ignore the other 50 tools until you need them.

**Does Semrush work for local SEO?**
Yes. Semrush's local SEO toolkit manages listings, tracks local rankings by city/zip code, and handles Google Business Profile optimization. It's a full local SEO solution.

**What happened to BeRush?**
Semrush migrated their affiliate program from BeRush to Impact.com in 2023. The affiliate program still offers commissions of up to $200 per first purchase.
