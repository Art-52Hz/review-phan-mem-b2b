## Keyword lookups and SERP lookups are separate

The [official FAQ](https://mangools.com/faq), checked October 9, distinguishes a keyword/domain search from loading a SERP panel. Cached requests can be treated differently. It describes a rolling 24-hour reset starting from the first request, with current resources visible in the account.

For your buying decision, record both counters. A research session that starts with a small keyword list may need several result inspections before you can recommend a page. Avoid calculating capacity from the keyword counter alone.

Here is a planning exercise, not a measured consumption test:

1. Pick one real client brief and record the country and language.
2. Write the seed searches you expect to run.
3. Identify which shortlisted phrases need a fresh SERP inspection.
4. Record the account counters before and after the work.
5. Save the selected queries, rejected alternatives and reasons.
6. Compare the resources consumed with the proposed plan's actual allowance.

Do not assume every click costs a lookup, or that every cached panel is free indefinitely. Inspect the counters and current terms for your account.

## Check the invoice and account scope

Mangools' [plans and pricing FAQ](https://mangools.com/plans-and-pricing), checked October 9, describes recurring monthly or annual billing until auto-renewal is disabled. Its refund wording includes a 48-hour window and an exclusion for two consecutive subscriptions. Verify the full current conditions before paying; cancellation and a refund are different actions.

The [main FAQ](https://mangools.com/faq) says KWFinder is not sold separately, describes shared resources for sub-users and distinguishes separate KWFinder/SERPChecker API plans. Confirm what your selected account actually includes.

We are not quoting a universal price or daily paid-plan allowance here: a vendor headline, an annual equivalent and the amount due today can differ. Complete this record from the offer you see:

## When I would postpone an upgrade

Postpone paying if you cannot name the client deliverable, your existing free evidence answers the question, or you have not checked how much research one brief actually consumes. More keyword rows will not fix an unclear audience or several overlapping articles.

Also postpone if your buying reason is a promised ranking or affiliate income. Our recorded sessions demonstrate that research panels can be inspected; they do not demonstrate profitable customer acquisition.
