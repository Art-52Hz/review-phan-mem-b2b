## The real cost: day one and renewal

The headline monthly price only applies because you pay the whole term up front. Two numbers matter more:

1. **What you pay today.** KVM 2 is $210.96 for 24 months. KVM 1 is $155.76.
2. **What it renews at.** After the term, KVM 2 renews at $14.99/mo and KVM 1 at $11.99/mo for the same term length.

Over four years, KVM 2 bought on this deal and renewed once for 24 months costs about $210.96 + $359.76 = $570.72, or about $11.89 per month on average. Plan your budget on that number, not on $8.79.
