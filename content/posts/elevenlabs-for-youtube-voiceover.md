## Commercial rights and YouTube monetization

ElevenLabs says paid-plan output may be used commercially, while Free output is limited to non-commercial use with attribution. The terms still require compliance with its prohibited-use policy and applicable rights. Treat the subscription invoice and generation date as records for the audio, and do not use a Free sample in a monetized deliverable. Sources: [official terms of service](https://elevenlabs.io/terms-of-use), checked Nov 27, 2026; [billing and commercial-use documentation](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/administration/billing), checked Nov 27, 2026.

That license does not guarantee YouTube Partner Program approval. YouTube reviews whether the channel provides original, authentic value and can reject repetitive or mass-produced videos. Its current disclosure examples say cloning your own voice for voiceovers does not require an AI disclosure; realistic synthetic depictions of other people or events do. A label by itself does not reduce monetization eligibility. Sources: [YouTube monetization policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392), checked Nov 27, 2026; [YouTube AI disclosure rules](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491), checked Nov 27, 2026.

## FAQ

### How many YouTube videos can 30,000 credits make?

At one credit per character on the website, it covers six 5,000-character first generations. Reserving 2,000 credits per video for revisions reduces this example budget to four videos, with 2,000 credits left. Your script length and other product use change the count. Source: [ElevenLabs credit pricing](https://elevenlabs.io/pricing), checked Nov 27, 2026.

### Can I monetize a video made with the Free plan?

ElevenLabs says Free output is non-commercial. Generate the final voiceover while subscribed to a paid plan if you intend commercial use. Upgrading later does not change the Free plan's stated restriction. Source: [ElevenLabs terms](https://elevenlabs.io/terms-of-use), checked Nov 27, 2026.

### Does an AI voice stop a channel from being monetized?

An AI voice alone does not decide YPP eligibility. YouTube evaluates the channel's originality and value, including whether its uploads are repetitive or mass-produced. Source: [YouTube monetization policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392), checked Nov 27, 2026.

### Do I need to label an ElevenLabs voiceover as AI?

YouTube lists cloning your own voice for a voiceover or dub as an example that does not need disclosure. It requires disclosure when realistic altered or synthetic content makes a real person appear to say or do something they did not. Source: [YouTube disclosure rules](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491), checked Nov 27, 2026.

The current offer lists $99 for the first annual year through Dec 1, 2026, and $220 as the regular annual price. If that price remains at renewal, two years cost $319 and three cost $539 before taxes. Check the checkout renewal terms before paying. Source: [official ElevenLabs pricing](https://elevenlabs.io/pricing), checked Nov 27, 2026.
