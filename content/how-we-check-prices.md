AI Pro Freelancer compares software for freelancers on one question: **what will this actually cost you, and is it the right size for the job?** This page explains how we answer it, and where our checks stop.

## What we check on every money page

1. **Price from the official page.** We read prices on the provider's own pricing or checkout page, in USD before tax, and write down the date. That date is shown at the top of each article as "Prices checked".
2. **The full first invoice.** A "$6.49/mo" headline on a 24-month term is one upfront payment. We show that payment, not only the monthly figure.
3. **Renewal price and total cost.** We record the renewal rate and calculate 12, 24, 36 or 48-month totals from the published numbers. These are our calculations from official inputs, not checkout quotes.
4. **Limits that change the real price.** RAM and CPU per plan, credits or minutes per month, extra-user fees, licenses (for example Windows on a VPS) and refund rules, including what they exclude.
5. **Who each plan fits.** We match plans to concrete freelance jobs: a single MT5 terminal, a client WordPress site, a weekly voiceover, keyword research for a few clients.

## What we don't claim

- **We don't publish speed or uptime tests we didn't run.** Where we haven't measured performance, we say so and tell you how to test it yourself during a trial or refund window.
- **We don't invent coupon codes or "verified" counters.** If a deal needs no code, we link to the offer. If there is no sale, our [deals page](/deals/) says "Not live yet".
- **Rankings aren't paid.** Providers can't buy a position. Some links earn us a commission (see our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/)), and we include plans with no affiliate link when they fit better.

## When we update

Prices change often, especially around Black Friday. We recheck money pages when a provider changes its pricing page and at least once a month during sales. If you see a price that no longer matches, [tell us](/contact/) and we'll recheck it.
