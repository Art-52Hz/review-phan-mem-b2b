## Method and scope

This index selects the lowest monthly advertised Linux price captured for each of three providers in our dataset. All selected offers use a 24-month introductory term. RAM/USD means GB of advertised RAM divided by the introductory USD monthly rate. It is a capacity ratio, not a benchmark. The Hostinger renewal uplift is (11.99 / 6.49 - 1) * 100, rounded to one decimal place. It describes one entry plan only.

Hostinger explicitly lists renewal for the same term. UltaHost explicitly lists the same monthly renewal rate for two years. Contabo displays a promotional rate for the first 24 months and a crossed-out comparison rate; we do not label the comparison rate as renewal. Taxes, region choices, optional licenses and add-ons are excluded. Contabo setup charges were not established from the captured listing. Consult the source before quoting a final invoice.

The snapshot covers these advertised product families, not every VPS seller or configuration. We opened official pages in a real browser on the checked date. No account purchase or performance test was performed. We intend to review the dataset monthly; the checked date records the actual latest verification, and there is no automatic freshness claim.
