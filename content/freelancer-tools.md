Most freelancers do not need a big software stack. They need one tool per job that the client will pay for: a funnel and email list, a presentation that exports cleanly, a keyword shortlist for the next article, or charts and alerts for a trading workflow. This directory collects our buying guides for those tools, with the real plan prices, the limits that make people upgrade and the refund rules to check before paying.

Start with the job, not the brand. Write down what you have to deliver this month, who pays for the tool (you or the client) and whether a free plan covers a first project. Then read the review for the tool that fits, and compare the full project cost rather than the monthly headline.

## How to choose without overpaying

1. **Test on the free plan or trial first.** Systeme.io and TradingView have free plans; Beautiful.ai has a card-required trial. Build one real deliverable before you pay.
2. **Count the limit that matters to your work.** For KWFinder it is lookups per day, for TradingView it is alerts and indicators per chart, for Systeme.io it is contacts.
3. **Check the refund window before choosing a yearly plan.** Several tools refund only within a short window or not at all once paid.
4. **Bill the client for client-only tools.** If a tool only exists for one client project, put it on that project's invoice.
