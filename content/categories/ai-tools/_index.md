## Choose the job before the tool

Start with the deliverable your client needs. The guides below help you define requirements, test a representative sample and record unresolved questions before subscribing. They are not a ranked benchmark of every product listed in this category.

- **Produce narration:** use the [voiceover scorecard](/posts/voiceover-evaluation-scorecard/) to separate pronunciation and revision effort from export and usage requirements. If your shortlist is ElevenLabs and Murf, [compare the same brief](/posts/elevenlabs-vs-murf-ai-2026/) before choosing.
- **Write client content:** follow the [AI writing evaluation checklist](/posts/best-ai-writing-tools-2026/) to check source accuracy, editing work and acceptance against one brief. A polished draft still needs review.
- **Deliver editable slides:** use the [presentation handoff checklist](/posts/beautiful-ai-review-2026/) to confirm the receiving format and what the client can edit.
- **Evaluate a workspace task:** the [Notion AI workflow guide](/posts/notion-ai-review-2026/) helps check plan access, source fidelity, missing information and the final handoff. Documented features are not measured time savings.

## Put the full project cost beside the sample

Use the [project-cost guide and blank worksheet](/posts/freelance-tool-project-cost/) to record the payment due now, subscription allocation, required add-ons, revisions and active working time. Keep unconfirmed charges unknown rather than assuming zero.

For a voice project, the [Murf buying guide](/posts/murf-ai-review-2026/) explains Studio generation and revision questions; the [ElevenLabs checklist](/posts/elevenlabs-ai-review-2026/) covers its separate plan and credit checks. Do not treat different products' usage units as interchangeable.

Some linked articles contain disclosed affiliate links. Check each guide's observation date and the vendor's current terms for your intended client use. A blank worksheet, trial preview or attractive cover does not prove that a product passes your delivery requirements.
