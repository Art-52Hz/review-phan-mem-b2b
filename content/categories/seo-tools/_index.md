## Start with the question you need to answer

Choose a tool around a concrete task and the evidence needed to complete it. A keyword estimate, a content score and an observed website conversion answer different questions.

| Your task | Start here | Evidence to keep |
|---|---|---|
| Find a customer problem worth covering | [Keyword research for freelancers](/posts/keyword-research-for-freelancers/) | Target market, query, search intent and actual result pages |
| Compare candidate queries | [Keyword shortlist worksheet](/posts/keyword-shortlist-worksheet/) | Observation date, metric source, overlap and a distinct page decision |
| Evaluate content guidance | [Surfer evaluation checklist](/posts/surfer-seo-review-2026/) | The brief, source checks, editing time and unresolved requirements |
| Choose a wider client SEO toolkit | [Semrush capacity and reporting checklist](/posts/semrush-review-2026/) | Required sites, users, markets, exports and total commitment |

## Make research lead to an action

Record the affected URL, the customer question and the evidence behind a proposed change. Decide whether an existing page can answer the question before creating another article with overlapping intent.

For tracking, keep the same market, device and observation period when comparing results. A successful website deployment proves the change is live; index status, ranking movement, visitor behavior and qualified conversions need their own evidence.

## Buy capacity only when the current task needs it

Use the [project-cost worksheet](/posts/freelance-tool-project-cost/) to record the payment due now, necessary users and add-ons, and the time spent validating reports. Confirm access and current vendor limits before promising a client a particular export or reporting schedule.

The guides in this category are not a measured ranking of every listed product. Some contain disclosed affiliate links. Check each guide's revision date and scope; product approval, keyword demand and commission eligibility are separate decisions.
