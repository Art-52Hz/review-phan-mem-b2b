Choose the guide that matches the decision you need to make with your client. These resources distinguish a plan from a measured result and include blank worksheets where available.

- **Before buying tools:** use the [project cost worksheet](/posts/freelance-tool-project-cost/) to record subscription costs, client requirements and who approves spending.
- **Before transferring a WordPress site:** use the [client site transfer checklist](/posts/wordpress-client-site-transfer-checklist/) to map the receiving owner, dependencies and acceptance evidence.
- **Before handing over a newsletter:** use the [newsletter export checklist](/posts/newsletter-platform-exit-checklist/) to document permissions, export scope and the receiving owner's acceptance.
- **Before choosing hosting:** use the [freelancer VPS checklist](/posts/best-vps-for-freelancers-2026/) to define workload, operations and recovery responsibilities.

The articles below cover related freelance tools. Provider features and terms can change; check the cited documentation and final quote before committing. An empty worksheet is a planning aid, not proof of a completed test or a recommendation for every project.
