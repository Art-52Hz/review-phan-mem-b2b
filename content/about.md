AI Pro Freelancer helps freelancers and small teams choose software without overpaying. We compare VPS hosting, AI voice generators, SEO tools and trading platforms on the numbers that decide a purchase: the first invoice, the renewal price, the plan limits and who each plan actually fits.

## Who runs the site

I'm **Vincent Pham** (Phạm Quang Tùng), the founder and editor, based in Vietnam. I started AI Pro Freelancer in 2026 because hosting and SaaS pricing pages are built to show the lowest monthly figure, while the real cost sits in the term length, the renewal rate and the add-ons. Every guide on this site starts from that problem.

You can reach me directly at [contact@aiprofreelancer.com](mailto:contact@aiprofreelancer.com) or on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tung-pham-82309a1a6).

## What we cover

- **VPS and hosting:** Hostinger, UltaHost, Contabo and others, including Windows VPS for MT4/MT5 and servers for n8n, Ollama or game hosting.
- **AI voice:** ElevenLabs, Murf and similar tools, compared by credits, commercial rights and real cost per project.
- **SEO and marketing tools:** Mangools, Semrush, SE Ranking and Systeme.io.
- **Trading tools:** TradingView plans and the infrastructure traders run next to them.

Current offers are collected on our [deals page](/deals/).

## How we work

Prices and plan limits come from each provider's official pricing pages, and every article shows the date we last checked them. We show the full first payment and the renewal price, not just the headline monthly rate. Where we cite performance numbers, they come from published benchmarks of that exact plan, with the data linked. The full method is on [How we check prices](/how-we-check-prices/).

We use AI tools to help with research and drafting. Every price, limit and recommendation is checked against the provider's own pages before it is published, and we correct errors as soon as we find them.

## How the site is funded

Some links on this site are affiliate links: if you buy through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Providers cannot pay for a position or a verdict, and we recommend plans without an affiliate link when they fit better. Details are in our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).
