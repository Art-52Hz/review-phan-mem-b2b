AI Pro Freelancer publishes software guides for freelancers and small teams. Our focus is helping readers define a requirement, check current vendor terms and compare the cost of completing a real project.

The site is run by Vincent Pham in Vietnam. Our topics include AI tools, SEO software, hosting and productivity workflows.

## How to read our reviews

Our current editorial approach distinguishes three kinds of information:

| Information | What it can establish |
|---|---|
| Vendor documentation | A feature, plan rule or stated limitation on the date checked |
| Recorded product observation | What happened in the specific sample and setup described |
| Original checklist or planning example | A method for your own decision; not a test result or predicted return |

A source-based guide does not establish that we used every feature or compared audio, speed or reliability in a controlled test. Where no recorded test exists, our updated articles identify that limit instead of publishing a numerical winner.

Check the review scope, source links and update date in the article you are reading. We are revising older material to remove unsupported personal-testing claims, ratings and stale prices. An update date by itself does not prove that every product feature has been retested.

## How AI supports the work

AI tools assist with research organization, drafts, coding and original editorial images. Generated text and images do not establish product performance. A conceptual illustration should not be interpreted as a screenshot, customer testimonial or measured result.

Our publishing process includes checks for working internal links, required assets and recognized affiliate-link markers. These technical checks do not verify product accuracy, search rankings, conversions or revenue. Product-specific conclusions still need appropriate sources or recorded observations.

## Affiliate relationships and the business goal

Some links may earn a commission from qualifying purchases. A relationship does not establish that a tool fits every reader, and a public program page does not prove our account was accepted. Our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/) explains the distinction.

The project's business goal is 1,000 USD in monthly affiliate commissions. This is a target, not a claim of achieved income or a promise about the reader's earnings. We keep clicks, approved commission and money received separate when assessing progress.

## Corrections and questions

If a source is outdated or a guide does not resolve your question, [contact us](/contact/) with the article URL and the detail to check. Before purchasing, verify the current plan, billing commitment and requirements for your intended use directly with the vendor.
