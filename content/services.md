I may earn a commission if you buy through a referral link. You can buy directly using your own account or use my referral link; the price to you stays the same. My setup fee is separate, and using a referral link is optional.

### Who owns the accounts and pays the subscriptions?

You do. Hosting, domains, licences and voice credits are paid separately by you. These are independent services; naming a provider does not imply an official partnership.

### How do you access my project?

Use temporary, limited access where supported, or a supervised session. You enter passwords, payment details, trading credentials and secret keys yourself. Remove temporary access when we finish.

### Can you guarantee trading profits, uptime or a speed score?

No. I check the agreed setup and document the handoff. Market outcomes, provider availability and website performance depend on factors beyond this service.

### What if I need changes or ongoing help?

Each package lists its revision allowance. Revisions adjust the agreed delivery; new features, new scripts and ongoing support need a separate quote. Ask before ordering if your project falls outside the listed scope.
