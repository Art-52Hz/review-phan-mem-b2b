## How to tell a real deal from a renewal trap

- **Look at the full first invoice, not the monthly figure.** "$6.49/mo" on a 24-month term is one $155.76 payment.
- **Find the renewal line before you pay.** Most hosting sales renew at a higher regular rate.
- **Pick the shortest term you're sure about.** A bigger discount on 48 months is no bargain if you move in a year.
- **Screenshot the price page with the date.** It's your proof if the invoice doesn't match.
